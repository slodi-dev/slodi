# PlanWindow: variations

## What the view is for

The window is A7: one flokkur, the last fundur and the next few. It's the paper
**Fundarhugmyndir** sheet, where the scouts write down the next four meetings. A leader opens it
to ask **"what still needs sorting before the next few fundir?"** The timeline answers "what is
coming". The window answers "what's left to do for it". So these directions make **readiness**
the content: what's settled about each fundur and what's still open.

Most fundir are the weekly flokksfundur at the usual time, and once its liðir are set there's
nothing to do. The work sits in the fundir that differ: the útilega that needs a venue and kit,
the dagsferð with no plan yet, the fundur moved to Thursday. So the window says the rhythm once,
lets a ready routine fundur shrink to one line (in Verkefni), and marks every exception with
its type and its span (_fös 30. okt.–sun 1. nóv._).

The current component is the timeline cut to five chips, with nothing on them about what's left.

## What it has to convey, ranked

| #   | Information                                              | Why it ranks here                                                                         |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **What is still open**, per fundur, in words             | The work itself: "2 liðir óákveðnir", "Vantar staðsetningu", "15 mínútur umfram".         |
| 2   | **Which fundur is next**, and its date                   | Work on the nearest first. A8 says the far ones are _meant_ to be thin.                   |
| 3   | **How ready each one is**, at a glance                   | A tally of five checks, plus the fundur's status in the calendar Staða's marks (✓ ✎ ⧗ ?). |
| 4   | **What kind of fundur**: the weekly one, or an exception | An útilega or a moved fundur brings its own work (kit, venue, telling parents).           |
| 5   | **What to bring** (`needs`)                              | The kit list for the next fundur, the thing a leader forgets.                             |
| 6   | The last fundur                                          | Context only: a footnote or a "Búið" card.                                                |

### The five checks

Readiness is five things, each either settled or open:

| Check  | Settled                           | Open                                                  |
| ------ | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Efni   | the fundur isn't "?"              | "Efni fundar óákveðið"                                |
| Dagur  | a day: "fös 30. okt.–sun 1. nóv." | "Vantar dag (vika frá 2. nóv.)"                       |
| Staður | the venue                         | "Vantar staðsetningu"                                 |
| Liðir  | "6 liðir ákveðnir"                | "Engir liðir enn", "2 liðir óákveðnir"                |
| Tími   | "85 / 90 mín"                     | "105 / 90 mín, 15 mínútur umfram", "Engin tímaáætlun" |

An undated scratchpad drops _Dagur_: it has no dates by design, and asking for one on every
fundur would be noise.

**What the data can't say yet.** "2 liðir óákveðnir" needs the number of _decided_ liðir per
fundur. The grid contract has `item_count` but not liður status, which lives in the bench data.
The lab adds `decided_count` to its fixture. The calendar lab hit the same gap and proposed the
same field, so it's one the contract would grow once for both.

## The rules every direction keeps

- **One family with the calendar and the timeline** (`planFamily.tsx`): the Staða status marks
  and colours, the calendar's type icons for exceptions, the inverse surface for öll sveitin,
  and the swallow-tailed flokkur pennant.
- The tally is never alone. "4 af 5 klár" and the open items are always written next to it.
  A missing stroke is a faint dotted placeholder, and a closed gate (all five) turns success green.
- Each fundur is focusable, and its name carries title, date, venue, status and readiness:
  _"Hjálp í viðlögum (1/2), 3. nóvember kl. 19:30, Skátaheimilið, drög, 4 af 5 klár. 2 liðir
  óákveðnir"_.
- Dates from `lib/format.ts`; counts through `countIs` ("1 atriði opið", "2 liðir óákveðnir",
  "í 5 fundum"). Card flips respect reduced motion. 48px targets at phone width.

## A · Spjöld (index cards)

<Canvas of={Stories.CardsBusy} />

**Story it tells:** a box of index cards, one per fundur, with divider tabs that carry the date,
a tiny tally, and an icon for an exception. The card is ruled: a red header line, then a blue
line per check, open ones in bold. The cards behind it show as flat paper edges.

**Hierarchy:**

1. The card in front: date, title and status.
2. Its checklist, open items in bold, and _Taka með_.
3. The tabs: where the other fundir are, and roughly how ready (their tallies).

**Why it looks this way:** it's the closest to the paper sheet the leaders already think in
("ég hugsa um þetta blað"), and one card at a time suits the job of sitting down to sort one
fundur. The tabs are a real tablist: one Tab stop, ← → Home End flip the cards, and focus moves
with them.

**Trade-offs:** you only see one fundur's detail at a time. Comparing two means flipping. The
tab row overflows sideways past five fundir, or less on a phone. The flat-edge stack is decoration.

## B · Verkefni (to-do)

<Canvas of={Stories.TodoBusy} />

**Story it tells:** the open items are the content. Under each fundur there's a box for every
thing still to do, then one quiet line for what's settled ("Efni, dagur og tími klár") and the
kit. A routine fundur with nothing open shrinks to one line ending in "Allt klárt".

**Hierarchy:**

1. The open items, at body size with a box each.
2. The fundur they belong to: date, exception tag, title and status.
3. "4 af 5 klár" on the right, the settled line, the kit.

**Why it looks this way:** it answers the window's question the most directly: read down
it and you have the list of things to do this week. Collapsing ready routine fundir means the
page gets shorter as the work gets done.

**Trade-offs:** the boxes look like checkboxes but aren't controls. The fundur is what you
open, and you tick things off in the bench. That's the main risk, and it would need either real
actions per item or a different mark. A ready útilega still takes several lines, on purpose. It
has the weakest sense of time: everything looks equally urgent.

## C · Röð (strip)

<Canvas of={Stories.StripBusy} />

**Story it tells:** the next few side by side, like columns of a timetable. Each column's top
rule is the fundur's status colour (Staða). Readiness is a tally: a stroke for each settled
check, and the fifth closes the gate. Under it are the count and the first open thing.

**Hierarchy:**

1. The tallies across the row: which fundir are a closed gate and which are still sticks.
2. Each column's date (big day number) and title.
3. The first open item, and "og 2 atriði í viðbót".

**Why it looks this way:** a tally is a scout's way to count, and it reads at a glance without
being a progress bar. Five strokes is exactly the five checks, so a full gate means "ready".
Side by side, you can compare fundir without opening any.

**Trade-offs:** only the first open item is written, so you open the fundur for the rest. Five
columns need a wide screen; on a phone it scrolls with snap, one and a bit at a time. A tally
needs learning the first time (the text beside it covers that). An undated scratchpad has four
checks, so no gate.

## D · Næst (focus)

<Canvas of={Stories.FocusBusy} />

**Story it tells:** the next fundur as a front page, with the date big, its full checklist and
kit. The ones after it are a short list with a small tally and "3/5" each. The last fundur is a
footnote.

**Hierarchy:**

1. The next fundur: date, title, status, "4 af 5 klár".
2. Its checklist and _Taka með_.
3. "Svo": the rest, one line each, with exception tags.

**Why it looks this way:** most of the time the next fundur is the only one that needs work
today. Making it big and the rest small matches A8: the near one gets detail, the far ones just
need to exist. Side by side on a wide screen, stacked on a phone.

**Trade-offs:** the rest get very little. An útilega three weeks out that needs booking now is
one line. It's the most conventional layout of the four.
