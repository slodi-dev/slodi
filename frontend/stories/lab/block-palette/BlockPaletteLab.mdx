# BlockPalette lab

The BlockPalette is the dagskrárbankinn rail on the left of the Vinnubekkur, where a leader picks
blocks for a fundur. The shell is fixed: collections top left, blocks below them, the plan in the
main body, all in PlanShell's 296px column.

**State, 2026-10-19:** C · Card grid was picked. A Icons, B Minimal list and D By section have
been removed. This round compares four ways to lay out the collections area (top left) once it
holds the leader's own **albums**, with C's cards below.

Stories: **Lab → BlockPalette → Directions**. _Comparison_ shows the current rail first, then C1–C4.
Each variation has a default (in the shell, beside the bench), many albums, empty album, create
album (with a duplicate name, rename, and delete with Afturkalla), phone and dark. _Setja í albúm_
tests the card's picker, _C4 · Tabs — reorder_ the keyboard reorder, _Icon mapping_ is the icon
reference, and _Playground_ has controls.

## Why C · Card grid

- **The picture does the telling-apart.** A 64px tile (the block's image, or its theme's icon on
  a tint of its type) is big enough to read, so the leader scans by picture before reading.
- **It's for browsing.** A leader opens the rail most often not knowing yet what they want. Cards
  show enough to judge without opening anything.
- **It suits albums.** A grid of pictures already looks like an album, so a collection that's
  "ours" feels like a place, not a filter.

### What changed on the card

- **No side stripe.** The coloured edge was decoration standing in for meaning. The type now
  shows as an icon and its word ("Leikur", with dice), and as the tint behind the picture. The
  card's border is neutral.
- **The minutes sit on the picture**, bottom right, like the length on a video thumbnail. It's
  read with the picture and saves a line.
- **The title wraps and hyphenates** (`lang="is"`, `hyphens: auto`). It never truncates, because
  the end of an Icelandic title is often what tells two blocks apart.
- **Badges with icons:** "Þegar á fundinum" (a tick) and "Endurmat fylgir" (a note). They sit on a
  neutral tint, never coloured.
- **"Bæta við" plus a bookmark button** ("Setja í albúm…"). Both are 44px. The bookmark is filled
  when the block is in an album, and its accessible name says how many ("í 2 albúmum").
- **The picture is the drag handle**, marked with a grip. It's the biggest thing on the card,
  and "Bæta við" stays a plain click.
- **The six type chips became one select** ("Allar tegundir"), beside the search. The chips took
  two rows the cards needed once the collections grew.

## Albums

An **album** is a named list of blocks the leader keeps for a reason of their own: "Uppáhalds
leikir", "Rigningardagar", "Útilega haust", "Fyrir Drekaskáta". It holds **references, not
copies**: editing a block in the bank changes it in every album, and a block can be in several.

How albums differ from the built-in sources:

- **Sources say where a block comes from.** They are fixed and can't be edited. _Allt_ is the whole
  bank. _Félagið_ is what's shared in the félag. _Færnimerki_ is the badge programme. _Sveitin mín_
  is what the sveit made itself.
- **Albums say what a block is for.** They are made, named, ordered and deleted by the leader. A
  Félagið block and a Sveitin mín block can sit side by side in "Rigningardagar".
- **Sveitin mín stays a source.** It's authorship, not curation, so it sits with the albums under
  "Mitt" (C1, C4) but can't be renamed or deleted.

### Personal or shared with the sveit

The proposal is two owners:

- **Personal** (owned by a user): the default. "Mín uppáhalds" belongs to one foringi, and nobody
  else needs to see it.
- **Shared with the sveit** (owned by the workspace): every foringi in the sveit sees it and can add
  to it. That's the natural home for "Rigningardagar" or "Útilega haust", which the whole team
  builds up. It's marked with a people icon and "deilt með sveitinni" for screen readers.

A new album is personal. Sharing it is one deliberate step (a later "Deila með sveitinni"
action), never a side effect. Who may edit a shared album follows the workspace role: an
editor or above can add and remove blocks, and only the album's creator or an admin can rename
or delete it.

## The four variations

All four share the same album behaviour:

- **Create:** "+ Nýtt albúm" opens an inline name field. Enter creates the album and selects it,
  and Escape cancels. A duplicate name (trimmed, any case) is refused with "Þú átt þegar albúm sem
  heitir „…“", announced, and the typed text is kept. The limit is 60 characters.
- **Rename:** inline, with the same rules.
- **Delete:** at once, with no dialog, and "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds (rule 5: reversible acts
  don't confirm).
- **Add a block from its card:** the bookmark opens "Setja í albúm", with every album as a ticked
  or unticked checkbox and "+ Nýtt albúm" (which creates the album with the block already in it).
- **Empty album:** "„Jólafundur“ er tómt.", how to fill it, and "Sýna allan bankann".
- **Counts** go through `countIs`: "3 blokkir", "1 blokk", "14 albúm".
- **Feedback:** every change is said once in the rail's live region (rule 4).
- **Popovers** use the native `popover` attribute, so they're never clipped by the scrolling
  list. Escape closes them, and focus goes back to the button that opened them.
- **Selection** is a toggle button with `aria-pressed`, as in the current rail. A listbox would
  forbid the "…" button inside each row.

### C1 · Folder list: everything in view

**The story.** Two labelled groups, _Bankinn_ (Allt, Félagið, Færnimerki) and _Mitt_ (Sveitin
mín, then the albums). One row per collection, with an icon, the name and "3 blokkir".

**Hierarchy.** Group → collection name → count. The "…" (Endurnefna, Eyða albúmi) is quiet at the
row's end. "+ Nýtt albúm" sits on the _Mitt_ heading.

**Why.** It's closest to the current rail, which the user likes, and the counts answer "is there
anything in it?" before a press. Only _Mitt_ scrolls, because the bank's three rows never grow
and the albums do.

**Trade-offs.** It's the tallest: at 44px a row, three to four albums show before _Mitt_ scrolls,
and the area takes almost half the rail. Twelve albums means a lot of scrolling in a small box.

### C2 · Album covers: albums as photo albums

**The story.** The sources are a compact row of four tabs with icons. The albums are covers: a
2 × 2 mosaic of the first four blocks inside, or the album's icon while it's empty. Three
across, with the name and count under each.

**Hierarchy.** Cover picture → name → count. The selected album's actions (Endurnefna, Eyða
albúmi) appear as words in a bar under the covers, because a cover is too small for a "…".

**Why.** It makes the albums the thing you see. A mosaic tells "Útilega haust" (fire, tent) from
"Rigningardagar" (flag, knots, heart) without reading, and it echoes the card grid below.

**Trade-offs.** Names wrap at about 80px, so long names make tall covers. Six covers is the most
that shows before scrolling. The actions are one step further away (select, then act), and the
mosaic is only as good as the pictures in it.

### C3 · Chips + "Öll albúm…": the most room for cards

**The story.** One wrapping row of chips: the four sources, the first two albums in the leader's
order, the selected album if it isn't one of them, and "Öll albúm… +N". The panel holds every album
with rename, delete and create.

**Hierarchy.** What you use most is one press away. The rest is two presses away, behind a count
that says how many.

**Why.** It's the smallest collections area (about three chip rows, against nearly half the rail
in C1), so the most cards show.

**Trade-offs.** Most albums are out of sight, so they're easy to forget. Chip names truncate
(the full name is in the panel and in the count line). Which albums get a chip has to be
predictable: here it's the first ones in the leader's own order, not "most used", which would
shuffle under the leader's hand.

### C4 · Tabs: Bankinn · Mitt

**The story.** Two real tabs (`tablist`, arrow keys, Home and End). _Bankinn_ lists Allt, Félagið
and Færnimerki. _Mitt_ lists Sveitin mín and the albums, in an order the leader sets by dragging
the handle or pressing ↑ ↓ on it ("Rigningardagar fært í sæti 1 af 5").

**Hierarchy.** Whose (tab) → which collection → count.

**Why.** It splits "find something in the bank" from "go to my things", which are two different
moods. Each panel is shorter than C1's two groups together, and the order of the albums is the
leader's own.

**Trade-offs.** It's one more press to switch between a source and an album. The selected
collection can sit in the tab that isn't showing (the count line still names it). Reorder adds a
handle to every row, and with it a third target per row.

## The data it needs

Nothing here exists in the API yet. Favourites are hidden behind `FAVOURITES_ENABLED` (off), and
**sc-620** ("Build real favourites: backend table + endpoints, 'Mappan mín' in the sidebar") is
where they get a backend. The proposal: albums **are** the favourites model, generalised.
"Uppáhalds" is one album every user gets by default, and sc-620's _Mappan mín_ is the list of a
user's albums. One model then serves the sidebar and the rail.

### Tables

**`album`**

- `id`: uuid
- `owner_user_id`: uuid, nullable. Set for a personal album.
- `owner_workspace_id`: uuid, nullable. Set for an album shared with the sveit. Exactly one of the
  two owners is set (a check constraint).
- `name`: text, 1–60 characters. Unique per owner, case-insensitive (`lower(name)`), so the
  duplicate error can come from the server too.
- `icon`: text, one of a fixed set (`bookmark`, `star`, `cloud-rain`, `leaf`, `snowflake`, `flame`).
- `position`: integer, the order in the owner's list.
- `is_default`: bool. The one "Uppáhalds" album per user, which can't be deleted.
- `created_by`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, and `deleted_at` (soft delete, `SoftDeleteMixin`).
  Soft delete is what makes "Afturkalla" a restore rather than a re-create.

**`album_item`**

- `album_id`: fk, on delete cascade.
- `content_id`: fk to `content` (Program, Event, Task), so a bank item of any kind can be put in an
  album, not just a block.
- `position`: integer, the order inside the album (C2's mosaic shows the first four).
- `added_by`, `added_at`.
- Primary key `(album_id, content_id)`: a block is in an album at most once.

### Endpoints

- `GET /albums?workspace_id=…`: the caller's own albums plus those shared in the workspace,
  ordered by `position`, each with `item_count` and up to four `cover_items` (id, theme, kind,
  image) for C2.
- `POST /albums` with `{ name, icon?, workspace_id? }`: create. `workspace_id` makes it shared.
  A duplicate name returns 409.
- `PATCH /albums/{id}` with `{ name?, icon?, position? }`: rename or reorder. Reordering one
  album shifts the others on the server.
- `DELETE /albums/{id}`: soft delete. `POST /albums/{id}/restore` is "Afturkalla", within the
  undo window.
- `GET /albums/{id}/items`: the items, ordered.
- `PUT /albums/{id}/items/{content_id}` / `DELETE …`: put a block in or take it out. Both are
  idempotent, which suits a checkbox.
- `GET /content/{id}/albums`: which of the caller's albums an item is in. This is what the card's
  picker ticks, and it could be folded into the bank list as `album_ids` to save a request per card.

**Permissions** follow `check_workspace_access`. A personal album is owner-only. A shared album
can be read by any workspace member, its items changed by an editor or above, and renamed or
deleted by its creator or a workspace admin.

## Rules every variation follows

- "Bæta við" and dragging from the picture, both through the bench's own intents
  (`blockAsLidur`, `dragId.library`).
- Search, type and the collections stay in the same place. The count line names the selected
  collection: "Uppáhalds leikir: 3 blokkir".
- With no fundur selected, "Bæta við" is disabled, and the reason is visible text linked with
  `aria-describedby` (rule 9). "Setja í albúm…" still works, because albums don't need a fundur.
- 44px targets everywhere, with a visible 2px focus ring. Inputs are 16px.
- `--sl-*` tokens only. Filled controls use the `-text` tier, so they pass AA in light, dark and
  patrol.
- Transitions collapse to 1ms under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the reorder animation is
  switched off.

## Icons

See the _Icon mapping_ story. **Type:** Setning `Sunrise`, Dagskrá `BookOpen`, Leikur `Dices`,
Slit `Moon`, Endurmat `MessagesSquare`, Annað `Puzzle`. **Theme:** Útilíf `Tent`, Ratleikni
`Compass`, Hreyfing `Footprints`, Hefðir `Flag`, Umhyggja `Heart`, Matur `Flame`, Endurmat
`MessagesSquare`, anything else `Shapes`.

Sources and albums use their own icons (Library, Building2, Award, House for the sources; star,
cloud, leaf, snowflake, flame and bookmark for albums), so a collection is never mistaken for a
type or a theme. The one overlap is the flame, which is both the Matur theme and an album icon.
It's acceptable because a theme icon only appears on a card and an album icon only in the
collections area.

Colour is never the only carrier: WCAG 1.4.1, the patrol colours already mean age groups, and
shape survives greyscale and colour blindness. The type's colour survives only as the tint behind
the picture.
