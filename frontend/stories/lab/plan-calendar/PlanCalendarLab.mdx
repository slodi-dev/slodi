# PlanCalendar day entries: D4 · Stimplar

## Why Stimplar

Two rounds led here. From the first (Fáni, Dagbók, Miði, Staða, Tákn) the user picked **Staða**:
the month as a planning-status map, because the question a sveitarforingi opens the month for is
"what haven't I sorted yet?". From the four Staða variations (plain glyph, sewn-on badge,
campfire, passport stamp) the user picked **Stimplar**.

It keeps Staða's heatmap (green sorted, blue drafted, amber unsure, grey "?") and adds two things
the others lacked:

- **A ritual.** A fundur is _stamped_ when it's ready, the way a scout gets a stamp in a book.
  Finishing a plan feels like something.
- **Information.** The stamp's picture is the fundur's **theme** (compass for Ratleikni, tree for
  Útilíf …) or, for anything that isn't a flokksfundur, its **type** (tent, mountain, flag …). A
  month of compasses and no trees is a programme-balance signal for free.

It also speaks the same dashed "stamp" dialect as Alert, so the planner and the app's messages
look like one product.

## The month: a rhythm plus exceptions

Roughly 80% of a month is the same weekly fundur per flokkur (Refir mán 17:30, Ernir þri 19:30,
Uglur mið 19:30, Birnir fim 18:00). The rest are exceptions: a fös–sun útilega, a laugardags
dagsferð, a mót, a moved fundur, a week that falls out.

- **The usual fundur is one quiet line:** stamp, flokkur initial, title, no time. The times are
  printed once, in **Fundir vikunnar** above the grid.
- **Anything that breaks the rhythm is loud:** a bold title and a line saying why (its time,
  "Fært frá mið.", the date range, "+15 mín.").
- **A multi-day event is one stamped strip** across its days, with punched holes at each end like
  a ticket roll. Where it crosses a week row, the bar continues on the next row from a flat,
  dashed cut, marked "↳ … framhald". Only the first segment is a button: _"Haustútilega á
  Úlfljótsvatni, fös 30. okt.–sun 1. nóv., öll sveitin, Úlfljótsvatn, drög"_.
- **A fundur that falls out** gets a crossed-out stamp and "Birnir fellur niður · vetrarfrí",
  instead of vanishing.
- **Week notes** ("Vetrarfrí í skólum 22.–26. okt.", "Jólafrí") run along the bottom of their row.

## The stamp language

<Canvas of={Stories.StampStatesStory} />

Each state is a different **shape**, not only a different colour. A stamp is only drawn once it's
_earned_; until then the box shows how far the fundur has come.

- **Staðfest** (KLÁRT): inked: double rule, roughened edge, its picture, a slight tilt. The only pressed planning stamp. A month of these is a month that's ready.
- **Drög** (DRÖG): a dashed rule, the picture drawn in pencil grey. The plan exists and its picture is known, but it hasn't been pressed.
- **Óvíst** (ÓVÍST): a dotted outline, a faint pencil sketch, a small "?" in the corner. An idea, not a decision. Dotted, not dashed, so it can't be confused with drög.
- **Óákveðið**: an empty dotted box with a big "?". ADR-002 §3's first-class "?": a place for a fundur, with nothing in it yet.
- **Lokið** (LOKIÐ): smaller (80%), in border grey, no tint behind it. The past is reference. Stamped past weeks were too loud in the grid lab, so they recede.
- **Fellur niður** (FELLUR NIÐUR): pressed in grey, crossed out. A cancellation is a fact worth showing, and it's not the same as "not planned".

The words on the band only appear from the list-row size up (the phone day list, the key). In a
50–150px cell the frame, picture and "?" carry the state, and every entry's accessible name says
it in words ("…, drög", "…, lokið", "… fellur niður, 22. október: vetrarfrí").

**Craft:**

- **The ink edge** is an SVG filter: turbulence drives a small displacement, and a second, finer
  noise is used as a speckle mask, so no two edges are perfectly straight. There are no images,
  and the colour is a token. The band's word is drawn outside the filter, so it stays crisp.
- **The tilt** comes from the fundur's id (`rotationFor`, −9° … +6°). It's the same on every
  render and in every view, but neighbours differ, so a stamped month looks hand-stamped rather
  than tiled.
- **The thunk** is a quick press past full size, a settle, and a ring of ink spreading out. It's
  CSS on the staðfest state, so it plays when a stamp first appears or turns staðfest, and never
  on a re-render. It uses transform only, and is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Dark mode:** the inks are the `-text` tiers, which are the lighter steps in dark. There are no
  shadows or glows, and the knocked-out band word is the surface colour.

## Information hierarchy

1. **Status**, from the stamp's state and the entry's tint.
2. **What it is**: the title. A quiet line for the usual fundur, bold for an exception.
3. **Whose and when**: the flokkur initial, then the time and the reason it's an exception.
   The stamp's picture adds the theme.

## Trade-offs

- **Two things vary per stamp** (the picture and the state), so it's busier than a plain glyph.
  The tint does a lot of the status work at cell size.
- **Bands are illegible at cell size**, so the words only appear from the list row up. In the grid
  the states rely on shape: ink, dashes, dots, an empty box, a cross.
- **The picture needs `theme`**, which is optional in the contract. Without it every flokksfundur
  falls back to a flag.
- **Tilt costs a pixel or two** of alignment, and a thunk on page load is a small flurry. It plays
  once per stamp and never on re-render.
- **The rough edge is a filter per stamp.** That's fine at a month's 30–40 stamps; a season list
  of hundreds would want one shared filter in a `<defs>`.

## What the data needs (sc-646)

Checked against `services/plan.service.ts` and `docs/frontend/plan-api-contract.md` (on
`origin/release/vinnubekkur-v1`):

- **Recurrence.** No rule exists; a `Patrol` has `id`, `name` and `accent` only. The lab derives
  each flokkur's rhythm from the most common weekday and time among its fundir (at least three).
  A real `Patrol.meets: { weekday, time, venue }` or a series id would make it a fact.
- **`moved_from`.** "Fært frá mið." is inferred: the flokkur had nothing, not even a cancelled
  fundur, on its usual day that week.
- **`cancelled`.** `PlanStatus` has no "falls out", so today a cancelled fundur is deleted, which
  looks the same as "not planned yet". The lab adds `cancelled: { reason }`. `PlanWeek.note`
  already covers sveit-wide breaks.
- **`ends_at`** (or `ends_on`). There is `starts_at` only, and `span_weeks` counts weeks, not days.
  A fös–sun strip needs an end. The lab adds it.
- **`decided_count`.** Readiness is the fundur's own status. `item_count` is a total, so "4 of 5
  liðir decided" can't be shown yet.
- **Band rules.** The contract's "a band week holds no per-flokkur cells" and "one band per week"
  don't match real weeks: an útilega shares its week with four weekday fundir.
