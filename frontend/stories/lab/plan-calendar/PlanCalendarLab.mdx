# PlanCalendar day entries: variations

The Dagatal's grid works. What sits in each day doesn't: every entry is the same white rounded
card with a 3px coloured left stripe, a bold title, small grey "19:30 · Skátaheimilið" and a
liðir bar at the bottom. That's the stock look of a generated dashboard. In a month view it adds
up to a wall of identical cards: you can count them, but you can't tell at a glance which
evenings belong to which flokkur, which weekend is the útilega, or what still needs planning.

Each look below is a lab copy of the entry inside a lab copy of the month grid (the same table,
Monday first, trailing row trimmed). The fixtures are fixed to October and December 2026, with
today on mánudagur 19. október, so the hard cases stay on the same days: 1, 2, 4 and 5 entries in
a day, long titles, troop-wide events and past days.

## What a day entry has to say, ranked

From the data the grid contract actually carries (`PlanCell` / `PlanBand` in
`services/plan.service.ts`), ranked by what a sveitarforingi needs from a **month**, not from a
single fundur:

1. **Whose it is**: a flokkur (with its colour) or öll sveitin. The month question is "is every flokkur meeting every week, and when is the whole sveit together?" A flokkur keeps its colour across the Rist, Dagatal and Tímalína, so colour is the fastest key there is.
2. **What it is**: the title, and the type when it isn't a plain fundur. An útilega or dagsferð changes the shape of the month (kit, drivers, parents). A flokksfundur is routine. The title tells fundir apart; the type tells routine from exceptional.
3. **How decided it is**: staðfest / drög / óvíst / óákveðið ("?"). The planning question: "what haven't I sorted yet?" ADR-002 §3 makes "?" a first-class state, and A8 (progressive detailing) means the far end of a month is _supposed_ to be undecided, so it has to read calmly.
4. **When**: the start time. Needed to tell two entries on one evening apart, and to plan around school. Less important than the day, which the grid already gives.
5. **Past or to come**. The past half of a month is reference, not work. It should recede without disappearing.
6. **Over budget**: liðir add up to more than the fundur's minutes. A Bekkurinn problem. Worth a flag in the month view only when it's true, and only in the look that's about readiness.
7. **Where**: venue. Matters on the day, rarely for the month. It's in every entry's accessible name and in the phone day list, not in the cell.

**Left out:** theme, kit (`needs`), liðir count, the kind split (`segments`). Bench-level detail. The current chip's segment bar is the main thing these looks drop.

What the data **can't** say yet: how many liðir of a fundur are decided. `item_count` is a total,
and liður status lives in the bench data, not the grid. So "readiness" here is the fundur's own
status. A real "4 of 5 liðir decided" tally would need a `decided_count` on the grid contract.

## The rules every look keeps

- Each entry is a `<button>` whose accessible name is the whole story, e.g. _"Flokksfundur Arna:
  Hnútar og bönd, 21. október kl. 19:30, Skátaheimilið, drög"_. It adds _"20 mínútur umfram"_
  when over budget and _"lokið"_ when past.
- A day holds 3 or 4 entries (by look) before the rest fold behind **"+N fleiri"**, which expands
  the day in place.
- Below 40rem of its own width, the grid stops squeezing titles into 50px cells. Each day becomes
  a button with small markers in the look's own language, and the chosen day is listed under
  the grid in the look's full form (48px rows, with who, where and status in words).
- Colour comes from the vivid `--sl-color-patrol-*` fills with their `-foreground` (near-black,
  AA on every fill in both themes). A troop-wide event wears the inverse surface: the one colour
  no flokkur has.
- Colour is never the only carrier. Every look also has a letter, a shape, a word or a line style
  for the same fact.
- Today's circle is centred on the number and drawn in `--sl-color-primary` (white text, 5.2:1
  in light; near-black on green-400 in dark).
- Dates come from `lib/format.ts`; counts go through `countIs` ("1 viðburður", "5 viðburðir").

## A · Fáni

<Canvas of={Stories.RibbonBusy} />

**Story it tells:** who meets when. Every flokkur's evening is a flag in its own colour, so a
glance down a column shows Refir and Birnir on Tuesdays, Ernir on Wednesdays, Uglur on Thursdays,
and the black (white in dark) banners that belong to everyone.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. The flokkur, from the fill.
2. The title, in bold.
3. The time, ahead of the title. Status is a quiet extra: dashed stitching inside the flag means
   "not decided yet", and "?" means óákveðið.

**Why it looks this way:** a flokkur has a fáni, and a patrol colour is the identity scouts
already use. A solid fill reads at a much smaller size than a 3px stripe, so the entry can shrink
without losing who it belongs to. The swallowtail makes it a flag rather than a card. A
troop-wide event is a square banner instead, so shape as well as colour says "everyone". Past
flags are lowered: faded to the flokkur's pale tint, mixed halfway into the surface, with
secondary text.

**Trade-offs:** status is coarse. Drög and óvíst look alike (both are only "stitched on"), and
staðfest can't be told from drög across a room. Six vivid fills in a busy week are loud, and the
inverse troop banner is the heaviest mark in the month, in black in light mode and white in dark.
Yellow (Drekar/Ernir) and green (Drótt/Birnir) are close in lightness, so the initial in the phone
list and the accessible name do the work for colour-blind leaders. The clip-path means the focus
ring has to sit inside the flag.

## B · Dagbók

<Canvas of={Stories.AgendaBusy} />

**Story it tells:** what each evening holds, read like a page of a paper diary: time, then what,
with a small disc for whose.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. The time, at the left edge, in a column.
2. The title.
3. The flokkur (a disc with its initial; a disc of every flokkur's colour for öll sveitin).
   Status is the line's hand: **ink** (upright, dark) for staðfest, **pencil** (italic, lighter)
   for drög and óvíst, and "?" in place of the time for óákveðið.

**Why it looks this way:** no card at all. A month cell is about 150px wide; a card spends a lot
of that on padding, borders and shadow. One line per entry fits four before folding, so the busiest
week still reads. Ink vs pencil is how a leader marks a paper plan, so it needs no legend for the
people this is for. Past lines go grey and their disc turns hollow.

**Trade-offs:** titles truncate at one line ("Trönur og reipa…"), so it relies on the name, the
hover and the phone list for the rest. The flokkur is the weakest signal of the five looks: a
small disc with a letter. The italic pencil is subtle at 12px, so óvíst vs staðfest won't be seen
across a room. It's also the least "Slóði-branded": it could be any calendar.

## C · Miði

<Canvas of={Stories.TicketBusy} />

**Story it tells:** each evening is a ticket: when it starts, what it is, and whether it has been
stamped yet.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. The time, on a stub in the flokkur's colour.
2. The title, on the ticket body.
3. What's missing, written on the stub: **DRÖG**, **ÓVÍST** or **?**. A staðfest ticket's stub
   only has the time, and its edge is solid.

**Why it looks this way:** it borrows the scouting **stamp** language picked for Alert: a dashed
edge means "not stamped yet", a solid edge means done. The perforation between stub and body is
the same dashed line, so the metaphor holds together. Status is a word, not a colour, which makes
it the most colour-blind-safe look for status. Writing the missing step, rather than the state,
keeps confirmed tickets quiet and puts ink where work is left. Used (past) tickets have a grey
stub and no word.

**Trade-offs:** the stub costs 44px of a 150px cell, so long titles break mid-word more often
("Undirbúningsfun|dur…"). It's the tallest entry, so it folds at 3. "?" on its own on the stub is
terse. Troop-wide tickets differ from flokkur tickets only by the stub's colour.

## D · Staða

<Canvas of={Stories.ReadinessBusy} />

**Story it tells:** where the planning work is. The month becomes a heatmap: green is sorted,
blue is drafted, amber is unsure, and dashed grey is still a "?". The past goes plain, because
colour marks work that's left.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. How decided it is, from the tint and a glyph (✓ staðfest, ✎ drög, ⧗ óvíst, ? óákveðið).
   These are the spec's own status colours: confirmed → success, draft → info, tentative →
   warning, undecided → neutral with a dashed border and a literal "?".
2. The title.
3. The flokkur (an initial on its colour, "S" for öll sveitin), the time, and **+N mín.** in red
   when the liðir run over budget.

**Why it looks this way:** it answers the planner's real question for a month, "what haven't I
done?", and it's the only look that makes A8 visible: a healthy plan is green near today and
greyer further out, so the gradient itself shows progressive detailing working. A glyph sits next
to every tint so colour is never alone. Over budget only shows here, because only this look is
about readiness.

**Trade-offs:** the flokkur drops to a 15px chip, so "who meets when" (look A's whole story) is
hard to see. Status colours compete with patrol colours if both are loud, which is why the patrol
is shrunk. It's the boxiest look and closest to the current card. The tint is new, but it's still a
bordered rounded rectangle. Readiness is the fundur's status, not its liðir (see the note above).

## E · Tákn

<Canvas of={Stories.IconsBusy} />

**Story it tells:** what kind of month it is. The útilega and the dagsferð stand out by shape and
size, and the routine fundir sit quietly around them.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. The type, from the icon: a flag for a flokksfundur, people for a sveitarfundur, a clipboard
   for the foringjar's skipulagsfundur, a tent for an útilega, a mountain for a dagsferð, a tent
   and tree for a mót, party poppers for an uppskeruhátíð.
2. The flokkur, from the tile's colour (inverse for öll sveitin).
3. The time and title, in body text beside it. Big outings get a whole row with a larger tile and
   a two-line bold title.

**Why it looks this way:** shape carries type, so colour is free to carry the flokkur. The
exceptional events are the ones a leader plans around, so they get the most ink. This is the look
that survives shrinking best: on a phone the markers are the icons themselves. Status is the
tile's fill: solid once planned (staðfest or drög), outlined when óvíst, dashed with "?" when
óákveðið.

**Trade-offs:** in an ordinary month it's mostly flags, so the icon tells you little week to
week. Drög and staðfest look the same. A kvöldvaka or a varðeldur gets no campfire, because it isn't
a type in the contract. That would need a tag or a new `EventType`. Icons need learning, and the
legend matters more here than in any other look.
