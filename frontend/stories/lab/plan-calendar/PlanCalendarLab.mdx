# PlanCalendar day entries: the Staða family

## A month is a rhythm plus exceptions

A sveit almost always meets the same way every week: each flokkur on its own weekday at its own
time (Refir mán 17:30, Ernir þri 19:30, Uglur mið 19:30, Birnir fim 18:00). Around that rhythm
sit a few exceptions: a fös–sun útilega, a laugardags dagsferð, a mót, a fundur moved because of
a sveitarfundur, a week that falls out for vetrarfrí.

So roughly 80% of the entries in a month are the same recurring fundur, and they're the least
interesting thing on the page. The current Dagatal gives each one the same white card with the
time printed again, so the rhythm becomes a wall of cards and the exceptions drown in it. Every
variation here follows the same rules:

- **The usual fundur is one quiet line.** It has the status mark, the flokkur's initial and the
  title, and no time. The times are printed once, in the **Fundir vikunnar** legend above the
  grid.
- **Anything that breaks the rhythm is loud.** It gets a bold title and a second line saying why:
  its time, "Fært frá mið." for a moved fundur, the date range for a span, "+15 mín." over budget.
- **A fundur that falls out says so.** It shows a ⊘ mark with "Refir fellur niður" and the
  reason, instead of silently vanishing. A gap and a cancellation are different facts.
- **A multi-day event is one bar** across its days. When it crosses a week row, the bar
  continues on the next row, with a flat, dashed cut edge and "↳ … framhald". Only the first
  segment is a button, named e.g. _"Haustútilega á Úlfljótsvatni, fös 30. okt.–sun 1. nóv., öll
  sveitin, Úlfljótsvatn, drög"_.
- **Week notes** ("Vetrarfrí í skólum 22.–26. okt.", "Jólafrí") run along the bottom of their
  week row, so a quiet week explains itself.

## Why Staða

From the first round (Fáni, Dagbók, Miði, Staða, Tákn), the user picked **Staða**: the month as
a planning-status map. It answers the question a sveitarforingi actually opens the month for,
"what haven't I sorted yet?". It is also the only one where the A8 ramp (progressive detailing)
reads at a glance: green near today, blue and amber after that, dashed "?" further out. Colour
carries **status**; the flokkur is second, as a small initial. The four variations keep that
heatmap and change only the **mark** each entry wears, from plain to playful.

Common to all four:

- The tint is the spec's status mapping: staðfest → success, drög → info, óvíst → warning,
  óákveðið → neutral, dashed, "?".
- The mark always differs in **shape** per status, so status never depends on colour alone.
- Past entries keep their mark but lose the tint, because colour is for work that's left.
- Each entry is a button with the whole story as its name (_"Flokksfundur Arna: Kortalestur og
  GPS, 20. október kl. 19:30, Skátaheimilið, staðfest, 15 mínútur umfram"_).
- A day folds after 3 entries behind "+N fleiri".
- Below 40rem of its own width, each day is a 48px button with mini marks, spans become thin bars
  across the days, and the chosen day is listed below the grid in full. A span is listed as
  "Dagur 2 af 3".
- Dates come from `lib/format.ts`, counts from `countIs`, colours from `--sl-*` tokens only.
- Animation is limited to a stamp "thunk" and three flame flickers, transform only, and none under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## D1 · Staða

<Canvas of={Stories.StadaBusy} />

**Story it tells:** where the planning work is. Green is sorted, blue is drafted, amber is
unsure, and dashed grey is still a "?".

**Information hierarchy:**

1. Status, from the tint plus a glyph: ✓ staðfest, ✎ drög, ⧗ óvíst, ? óákveðið.
2. The title: a quiet line for the usual fundur, bold for an exception.
3. The flokkur initial, then the time and the reason it's an exception.

**Why it looks this way:** it's the picked look, adapted to the rhythm. The glyph is the smallest
mark that still separates four states without colour, so the tint and the title carry the cell.

**Trade-offs:** the most sober of the four. Nothing here is fun, and the glyphs are small at 13px.
It's still a bordered rounded rectangle, though the tint and the quiet routine lines keep it from
reading as the old card.

## D2 · Merki

<Canvas of={Stories.MerkiBusy} />

**Story it tells:** every fundur earns a sewn-on badge as it's planned. A month full of stitched
checks is a month that's ready.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. Status, as a round badge with a solid edge and a dashed inner ring (the embroidery). The badge
   holds a check (staðfest), a pencil (drög) or a question mark (óvíst). An empty, dashed badge
   means óákveðið, because nothing has been sewn on yet.
2. The title.
3. The flokkur, as a small swallowtail flag tab in its colour, with its initial, on the badge's
   shoulder. A troop-wide event's tab is black with "S" (white in dark).

**Why it looks this way:** merki are what a scout collects and sews on, so "earning the badge" is
a metaphor the people using this already live with. The flag tab carries the patrol colour
without competing with the status tint.

**Trade-offs:** the badge is the widest mark (24px plus the tab), so titles get less room. The
tab's initial is 10px. On a phone the badge shrinks to 16px and loses its stitching and its tab,
so it becomes D1 with a circle.

## D3 · Varðeldur

<Canvas of={Stories.VardeldurBusy} />

**Story it tells:** the fire grows with the plan. The month shows where the fires are already lit
and where there are still only sticks.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. Status as a fire that grows:
   - unlit crossed sticks (óákveðið), a small inline SVG;
   - a spark (`Sparkles`, óvíst);
   - a flame (`Flame`, drög);
   - a fire on logs (`FlameKindling`, staðfest), which flickers three times when it appears.
2. The title.
3. The flokkur initial and the time. Multi-day útilegur, mót and dagsferðir also carry a round
   **tent / tent-and-tree / mountain badge**, so a span says what kind of weekend it is.

**Why it looks this way:** the campfire is the centre of scouting, and "getting the fire going"
is exactly what planning a fundur feels like. The four steps read as a sequence even to someone
who never opens the key. That's the biggest advantage over a set of unrelated icons.

**Trade-offs:** spark vs flame is the subtlest pair at 15px. The fire on logs is busy at small
sizes. The metaphor implies urgency ("the fire is out!") that an óákveðið fundur three weeks away
doesn't deserve, so the dashed grey tint has to keep "?" calm. The flicker is decoration and must
stay off under reduced motion, which it does.

## D4 · Stimplar

<Canvas of={Stories.StimplarBusy} />

**Story it tells:** a passport of fundir. A ready fundur gets its stamp, and the month fills up
like a stamp book. Undecided ones are empty boxes waiting.

**Information hierarchy:**

1. Status as the stamp's state:
   - **staðfest:** pressed, rotated −8° with a solid dashed rubber edge, and a short "thunk" as it
     lands;
   - **drög:** lined up but not pressed (straight, dashed);
   - **óvíst:** a dotted outline;
   - **óákveðið:** an empty dotted box with "?".
2. The title.
3. The stamp's picture, which is the fundur's **theme** for a flokksfundur and its **type** for
   anything else (see the key). Then the flokkur initial and the time.

**Why it looks this way:** it reuses the dashed "stamp" language we picked for Alert, so the
planner and the app's messages speak the same visual dialect. The icon carries real information
the other three don't: what the fundur is _about_. A month of Ratleikni compasses with no Útilíf
tree is a programme-balance signal for free.

**Trade-offs:** two things vary per stamp (picture and pressing), so it's the busiest. The rotation
costs a pixel or two of alignment. The theme icon needs `theme`, which is optional in the contract;
without it every flokksfundur falls back to a flag. Drög vs óvíst (dashed vs dotted) is subtle,
so the tint does most of that work.

## What the data needs

Checked against `services/plan.service.ts` and `docs/frontend/plan-api-contract.md` (on
`origin/release/vinnubekkur-v1`):

- **No recurrence.** The contract has individual events only; a `Patrol` carries `id`, `name`
  and `accent`, with no weekday or time. The lab **derives** each flokkur's rhythm: the most
  common weekday and time among its fundir, needing at least three. That works for a full
  starfsár but not for a new or sparse one. The real fix is a rule per flokkur (e.g.
  `Patrol.meets: { weekday, time, venue }`), or a series id on generated fundir.
- **No moved-from.** "Fært frá mið." is inferred: the flokkur had nothing, not even a cancelled
  fundur, on its usual day that week. A `moved_from` date, or the series link above, would make
  it a fact.
- **No end.** There is `starts_at` but no `ends_at`, and `span_weeks` counts weeks, not days. A
  fös–sun útilega can't be drawn as fös–sun without an **`ends_at`** (or `ends_on`) on
  `PlanBand`/`PlanCell`. The lab adds it as a lab-only field.
- **No "falls out".** `PlanStatus` is `unknown | tentative | draft | confirmed`. A cancelled fundur
  can only be deleted, which makes it indistinguishable from "not planned yet". The lab adds
  `cancelled: { reason }`. A status `cancelled` (or `fellur_nidur`) with a reason would do.
  `PlanWeek.note` already covers whole-sveit breaks ("Vetrarfrí", "Jólafrí") and is used as-is.
- **Band weeks vs the rhythm.** The contract says _"a band week holds no per-flokkur cells"_ and
  _"one band per week"_. Real weeks contradict both: a fös–sun útilega sits in the same week as
  four ordinary weekday fundir, and mið 28. here has a foreldrakynning and a skipulagsfundur
  alongside a flokksfundur. The month view handles it, but the Rist's band rule needs revisiting.
- **Readiness is the fundur's own status.** `item_count` is a total and liður status lives in the
  bench data, so no variation can show "4 of 5 liðir decided" without a `decided_count`.
- **Theme** (`theme`, optional) drives D4's stamps. It's already in the contract.
