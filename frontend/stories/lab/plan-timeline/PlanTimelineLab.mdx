# PlanTimeline: variations

## What the view is for

A flokksforingi opens the timeline to ask **"where are we in the term, and what is coming
up for my flokkur?"** It's Signý's view from the user research (#21): one flokkur, time running
along, "hvernig þetta keyrir". The grid answers "is every flokkur covered this week?" and the
calendar answers "what happens on which day?". The timeline answers how one flokkur's term is
going: what's behind, what's next, where the gaps are, and which weeks aren't ordinary.

Most weeks are the same: one flokksfundur on the same evening at the same time. The things a
leader plans around are the **exceptions**: a fös–sun útilega, a dagsferð on a Saturday, a
sveitarfundur, a fundur moved to another evening. So every direction says the weekly rhythm
**once** ("Vikulegur fundur á þriðjudögum kl. 19:30"), draws the routine fundir quietly, and
gives the ink to the exceptions. A multi-day event is one span: _fös 30. okt.–sun 1. nóv._

The current component is a month-grouped list of the same rounded chips as everywhere else.
Every fundur looks the same, today isn't marked, and a gap in the term is invisible.

## What it has to convey, ranked

| #   | Information                                                        | Why it ranks here                                                                                       |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Where today is**, and the next fundur                            | The first question on opening any plan. Everything before it is reference.                              |
| 2   | **The exceptions**: útilega, dagsferð, sveitarfundur, moved fundir | These shape the term: kit, drivers, parents, a different evening. They're what the leader plans around. |
| 3   | **How decided each fundur ahead is**                               | The planning question, in the calendar Staða's language: ✓ staðfest, ✎ drög, ⧗ óvíst, ? óákveðið.       |
| 4   | **The sequence and the gaps**                                      | Weeks with nothing on them, and a badge running over two weeks.                                         |
| 5   | **The rhythm**: the usual evening and time                         | Said once. A routine fundur only needs its date.                                                        |
| 6   | Titles of routine fundir                                           | Needed to tell fundir apart, but not to plan the term.                                                  |
| 7   | Past fundir                                                        | Context. They recede and lose their status mark, as in Staða.                                           |
| —   | Venue, kit, liðir, budget                                          | Bench and window detail. In the accessible name where it helps, not on the timeline.                    |

## The rules every direction keeps

- **One family with the calendar and the window** (`planFamily.tsx`, copied in both labs):
  - status uses the calendar **Staða** marks and colours: ✓ success, ✎ info, ⧗ warning,
    and a dashed neutral "?" for óákveðið. There's always a glyph with the colour;
  - exceptions use the calendar's type icons: tent for útilega, mountain for dagsferð, people
    for sveitarfundur, clipboard for skipulagsfundur, party poppers for uppskeruhátíð;
  - troop-wide events wear the inverse surface, "the one colour no flokkur has";
  - the flokkur is a swallow-tailed pennant with its initial, the calendar's Fáni shape.
- Each fundur is a `<button>` with the whole story as its name, the same shape as the calendar's:
  _"Útilega: Haustútilega á Úlfljótsvatni, 30. október til 1. nóvember kl. 17:00, öll sveitin,
  drög"_, with _"breyttur tími"_ for a moved fundur and _"lokið"_ for a past one.
- Dates come from `lib/format.ts` (weekday abbreviations are the lab's own, as in the calendar
  lab). Counts go through `countIs` ("1 vika án fundar", "8 fundir búnir").
- Vivid `--sl-color-patrol-*` with its `-foreground`. Every 48px target holds at phone width.
  Nothing moves.
- The rhythm is the most common weekday and time among the flokkur's own flokksfundir. It only
  counts if at least three fundir and half of them share it. Without one, every flokksfundur is
  routine and only the event type marks an exception.

## A · Slóð (trail)

<Canvas of={Stories.TrailBusy} />

**Story it tells:** the term is a route. The walked part is a solid line; ahead is dashed. The
flokkur's flag is planted at Í dag. Routine fundir are small waypoints on the path. Exceptions
are camps: a square with the type's icon. A gap on the path is "2 vikur án fundar".

**Hierarchy:**

1. The flag at Í dag, and the next fundur under it (bigger, filled with the flokkur's colour).
2. The camps: útilega, dagsferð, sveitarfundur, with their span and time.
3. The routine fundir: date and title on one line, with a status mark ahead and none behind.

**Why it looks this way:** a scout already reads a route. Solid against dashed says
walked against not walked without a legend. Ahead, the waypoint ring is the status colour, so
the gradient from green near today to dashed grey further out shows A8 (progressive
detailing) along the path. Routine stops shrink because the weekly fundur is the path itself,
not a place on it.

**Trade-offs:** it's long. A 20-week term is a tall page, and on a wide screen most of the
width is unused. It shows one flokkur only. The camp icons need the word beside them the first
few times.

## B · Kvarði (ruler)

<Canvas of={Stories.RulerBusy} />

**Story it tells:** time runs left to right, as Signý drew it. Weeks are ticks on a ruler, and
each fundur hangs off its week on a string. A thin string is the weekly fundur; a heavy one is
an exception, tagged with its type. The string's colour is the status. It opens scrolled so
last week is at the left edge.

**Hierarchy:**

1. The Í dag line, and the strings to its right.
2. Exceptions: heavy strings, the type and the span, in the inverse colour when it's for the
   whole sveit.
3. Routine titles: just the title, because the tick already gives the date.

**Why it looks this way:** it's the only direction with time on the x-axis, which is how the
person who asked for a timeline pictured it. Spacing is honest: a two-week badge is two weeks
wide, and an empty week is an empty tick.

**Trade-offs:** on a phone you see two and a half weeks at once and scroll sideways for the
rest. Stacking two fundir in a week works, but titles wrap in a 7.5rem column. Status rides on
string colour plus a small glyph, so it's quieter here than in Staða.

## C · Dagbók (diary)

<Canvas of={Stories.DiaryBusy} />

**Story it tells:** a hand-kept diary with a ruled line per week, every week of the term, so
an empty week is an empty line. The usual time is a ditto mark (〃 = "same as usual"). An
exception writes its icon in the time column and its type and span under the title. The
week's ATH note sits on its line.

**Hierarchy:**

1. The Í dag week, ruled off above and below with a tab in the margin.
2. Exceptions: an icon where the ditto would be, a bold title, the type and span.
3. Routine lines: 〃 and the title. Notes and "framhald" lines in small italics.

**Why it looks this way:** the ditto mark says the rhythm on every routine line without
repeating "19:30" twenty times, and any line that isn't a ditto is worth reading. It's the only
direction that shows the week notes (öskudagur, vetrarfrí), which matter when planning a week.
The red margin rule makes it read as paper.

**Trade-offs:** it's the densest. The status mark is small, and the eye goes to exceptions
first, then to status. It's the closest to a plain list, so it says the least about the
_shape_ of the term. The calendar dropped its own Dagbók look. This one stays because the week
row, unlike a day cell, has room.

## D · Brautir (swimlanes)

<Canvas of={Stories.LanesBusy} />

**Story it tells:** every flokkur on one time axis. The chosen one is tall and on top, the
others are context below, and the whole sveit has its own lane at the top. The weekly fundur
is the flokkur's pale tint with a thin edge of its colour. An exception, or a moved fundur, is
the full vivid colour, so the eye lands on it. Clashes and gaps across the sveit show at once.

**Hierarchy:**

1. The vivid blocks: exceptions and moved fundir, by flokkur.
2. The chosen flokkur's lane: its titles in full, with status marks.
3. The other lanes: one line each, truncated, for comparison.

**Why it looks this way:** "rhythm and exceptions" is easiest to see when the rhythm is pale and
continuous and the exceptions break it. Patrol colour carries identity here, so status keeps to
the glyph (in the fill's own ink on a vivid block, where the status colours wouldn't reach AA).

**Trade-offs:** it's the grid again, turned on its side. It loses what makes a timeline a
timeline: one flokkur's story. It's wide, and on a phone it's mostly sideways scrolling. Other
lanes' titles truncate. Two of the six patrol fills (yellow and green) are close in lightness,
so the pennant initial and the name carry the flokkur for colour-blind leaders.

## Edge states

<Canvas of={Stories.Undated} />

The scratchpad has no dates, so there's no Í dag and no past, and the axes count weeks instead.
With no times there's no rhythm to break, so every flokksfundur is routine and only the other
event types stand out.
