# SplitGrid: purpose and directions

SplitGrid is what a section of a fundur turns into after "Skipta á flokka": one lane per flokkur,
running at the same time. It sits inside the fundur sheet (see **Lab → FundurSheet**, which is
being redesigned alongside this), in place of the section's list of liðir.

All four directions here are live. Every move dispatches the real `benchReducer` intents
(`laneMoveAcross`, `laneMove`, `laneAdd`), so the announcements are the bench's own, and the drag
uses the same `dragId` vocabulary and collision rule as `BenchProvider`. The lab adds only what
the merge proposal needs (`labMerge`, `labRestore` in `labBench.tsx`).

## 1. Why a leader splits a section

The sveit splits up for part of the evening and comes back together. Typical reasons:

- **A stöðvaleikur.** Four stations (hnútar, áttaviti, skyndihjálp, eldfæri), and each flokkur
  starts at a different one and rotates. This is the most common case, and the default fixture.
- **Different levels.** The older flokkur does a harder version, or something else entirely.
- **Flokkastarf.** Each flokkur plans its own 30 minutes.
- **Space or gear.** There is one prímus station, so only one flokkur can be there at a time.

What they all share: **the lanes run at the same time, and the section ends when the last flokkur
is done.** "Lengsti flokkur ræður."

## 2. What the leader needs to see, ranked

1. **Which flokkur does what, and when.** This is the plan itself. It has to be readable at the
   fundur, from a phone, between activities.
2. **Whether the lanes are balanced.** The longest lane sets the section's length and moves
   everything after it. A flokkur that finishes 20 minutes early is 20 minutes of kids with
   nothing to do. Waits have to be visible as waits, with a number, and the lane that sets the
   length has to be named.
3. **Clashes** (stöðvaleikur only). Two flokkar at the same station at the same time is a
   mistake that the current grid can't show at all.
4. **How to move a liður to another flokkur.** That's the main edit in a split section. It
   needs to work by keyboard (← →), by drag, and by a visible button, and it has to be
   announced.
5. **What merging does.** Rare, but consequential, and today it's silent about what it keeps.

Secondary: theme, status, venue. None of the directions show them on the block. They belong in
the liður editor.

**Patrol identity** is always the vivid patrol colour _and_ the name together: a coloured name
tag on every lane, plus a colour edge on every liður. Colour is never the only cue. Two patrols
with similar colours, or a colour-blind leader, still read the name.

## 3. The current grid, briefly

Time runs down the side and there is one column per flokkur, so liðir are as tall as they are
long and the idle tail shows as "N mín laus". That works for two to four flokkar on a laptop. It
breaks in three places:

- **Six flokkar on a phone** gives six columns about 50px wide. Titles wrap a letter at a time.
- **A stöðvaleikur** can't be read as one. The question "who's at Skyndihjálp at 20:10?" means
  scanning every column.
- **"Sameina í eitt"** keeps only the first flokkur's lane and drops the rest without saying so.
  The reducer's comment says "it takes one and says so", but the button doesn't say it.

## 4. The directions

### A · Time axis

<Canvas of={Stories.TimeAxisUneven} />

**The story it tells:** "Here is the evening on one clock. Ernir are standing around for 30
minutes."

**Hierarchy:**

1. Rows of liðir, each as wide as it is long.
2. Hatched gaps labelled "30 mín bið".
3. Each lane's name tag and total, with "ræður lengd" on the longest lane.
4. A shared ruler and "Allir saman aftur kl. 20:55".

**Why it looks this way:** it turns the current grid on its side. Rows stack on a phone, where
columns can't, and a horizontal bar is the shape people already read as "duration" (the
FundurSheet lab's bar direction uses the same idea). Waits are hatched, the same pattern the
sheet uses for overrun, so balance is something you see before you read it. A gap is a button:
a wait is somewhere to put something.

**When to use it:** whenever balance is the question, which is most of the time when planning.
It's the strongest general replacement for the current grid.

**Trade-offs:**

- Short liðir on a phone get a one-line, cut-off title ("Hnú…"). The full name is in the
  button's accessible name and in the selection strip once it's tapped. At six flokkar × six
  10-minute liðir it's legible as a pattern, not as text.
- ← → moves to the previous or next flokkur, which is up and down on screen in this layout. We
  kept ← → in every direction so leaders learn one rule, and the strip's buttons name the
  flokkur, but it's less spatial here than in the current grid.

### B · Stations

<Canvas of={Stories.StationsUneven} />

**The story it tells:** "This is the rotation. Refir start at Hnútar, then go to Áttaviti… and
at 20:10 Ernir and Uglur are both at Hnútar."

**Hierarchy:**

1. Stations as rows, time slots as columns.
2. The flokkar as coloured, named tokens in the cells.
3. Clashes: an amber cell, the word "Árekstur", and a count above the table.
4. The flokkar strip with totals and waits (also the drop targets).
5. A "Bið" row showing who is idle in each slot.

**Why it looks this way:** a stöðvaleikur is planned as a rotation chart on paper, with stations
down the side and groups moving across. It's the only direction that answers "who is at this
station?" in one look, and the only one that can show a clash. The tokens are the vivid patrol
colour with the name in dark text, so they can be read from across a room.

**When to use it:** when the lanes share stations, as in stöðvaleikir or a rotation of activity
tables. It's also the most useful view right after "Skipta á flokka": every lane is a copy, so
every station clashes (story _B · Stations: just split_), which tells the leader exactly what
to rotate.

**Trade-offs:**

- When the flokkar do unrelated things, every liður becomes its own station and the table turns
  into a sparse diagonal. Use A or C for that.
- Slot columns come from every lane's start and end times, so uneven lengths produce narrow
  slots (the 5-minute "20:50" column). The table scrolls sideways on a phone, with the station
  column pinned.
- A liður that spans two slots shows as a token and then "frh.". Only the first is focusable.

### C · Matrix

<Canvas of={Stories.MatrixUneven} />

**The story it tells:** "Each flokkur's evening, row by row, on one timetable." It reads like the
Rist, with flokkar down the side.

**Hierarchy:**

1. One row per flokkur.
2. Cells for each liður, spanning a fixed number of time slots.
3. A "Samtals" column with the total, the wait, and a bar against the longest lane.
4. Slot times in the header.

**Why it looks this way:** it's the densest direction and puts text first. Titles wrap in full,
start times are printed in each cell, and the totals column lines the lanes up for comparison
without reading the whole row. The slot size is the largest one every liður fits exactly (never
under 5 minutes), so cells in the same column start at the same time. It's a real `<table>`, so
a screen reader can move by row (flokkur) and column (time).

**When to use it:** printing, or a projector at a foringjafundur, or six flokkar with many short
liðir, where A's blocks get too small to read.

**Trade-offs:**

- It's not proportional in the way A is: a column is a slot, not a minute. Balance is read from
  the totals column more than from the shape.
- On a phone it scrolls sideways (about 6rem per 10 minutes).
- One odd-length liður (5 minutes among 15s) makes the slots 5 minutes wide and the table three
  times wider.

### D · Cards

<Canvas of={Stories.CardsPhone} />

**The story it tells:** "Here's what Refir do tonight, in order. The other flokkar are one tap
away, and you can see their totals."

**Hierarchy:**

1. The open flokkur's liðir as full-width cards, with times, full titles and minutes.
2. The end card: "Lok 20:50 · 5 mín bið þar til allir eru búnir", or "þessi flokkur ræður
   lengdinni".
3. The switcher: every flokkur's tag, total, wait and a bar against the longest lane.

**Why it looks this way:** it's built for the phone at the fundur. A flokksforingi only needs
their own flokkur's plan, so one lane at a time gives every title room to wrap. The switcher
keeps the balance check on screen, because each tab's bar and "30 mín bið" are the comparison.
On a wide screen the switcher becomes a column on the left.

**When to use it:** on a phone, and for handing a flokkur its own running order. It's the phone
form for whichever desktop direction is picked.

**Trade-offs:**

- You can't see two lanes at once, so moving a liður "to where Ernir have a gap" means checking
  the Ernir tab first.
- When a liður is moved with the keyboard or a "Færa til" button, the view follows it to its new
  flokkur, so a second → keeps moving it. A drag onto another tab doesn't follow, because the
  leader is still working on this lane.

## 5. What every direction does the same

- **Moving between flokkar:** ← → on a focused liður moves it to the previous or next flokkur,
  ↑ ↓ reorders it within its lane, and focus follows the liður. A drag onto another lane (A and C:
  the row; B: the flokkur's chip; D: its tab) does the same, using the bench's dnd-kit setup.
  A tapped liður shows a strip with a named button for every other flokkur ("Færa til: Ernir,
  Uglur…"), so a phone can send a liður from the first flokkur to the sixth in one tap. Every move
  is announced by the reducer: "Liður færður milli flokka: … Fyrri flokkur: Refir. Nýr flokkur:
  Ernir."
- **Balance sentence:** "Lengsti flokkur ræður: Uglur (75 mín). Bið: Refir 5 mín · Ernir 30 mín ·
  Birnir 15 mín." Or "Jafnt: 4 flokkar, allir 60 mín." It uses `countIs` and never inflects a
  patrol name. Names are user data, so they're always in the nominative after a colon.
- **Edge cases:** 2 to 6 flokkar, an empty lane ("Ekkert skráð · + Bæta við"), long titles and a
  long flokkur name, and an undated fundur, where clocks become "+15 mín" and cards show "2.
  liður". See each direction's _2 flokkar, long titles, undated_ and _empty lane_ stories.
- **Tokens only.** The patrol colour comes in as `--lane` / `--lane-on`. The vivid colour is only
  ever a background behind its own `-foreground`, an edge, a bar, or a 14% tint under primary
  text, which gives AA in light and dark. Touch targets are 48px when the box is phone-narrow.
  Transitions only run under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

## 6. Merging: "Sameina í eitt" that says what it keeps

<Canvas of={Stories.Merge} />

Today the button keeps the first flokkur's lane and drops the others in silence. The leader finds
out later, if they notice at all.

**Proposal:**

1. **If the lanes are identical,** for example right after a split, nothing is lost. Merge at
   once and offer "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds (UX rule 5: reversible acts happen at once, with
   undo). The button reads "Sameina í eitt".
2. **If they differ,** the button reads "Sameina í eitt…" (the ellipsis means a question
   follows) and opens an inline confirmation under the header. Focus moves to its heading. It
   offers two choices:
   - **Halda öllu, hvað á eftir öðru** (the default). Every distinct liður that any flokkur has is
     kept once, in the first flokkur's order, followed by whatever the others had beyond that. A
     liður one flokkur has twice is kept twice. The preview lists each liður with the flokkar
     that had it, and gives the cost in time: "6 liðir í einni röð, 85 mín í stað 75 mín
     samhliða. Fundurinn lengist um 10 mín."
   - **Halda einni dagskrá** plus a choice of flokkur. Under it is a red list, "Fellur út: 3
     liðir · 40 mín", with each dropped liður and whose it was. The confirm button turns red and
     says so: "Sameina og sleppa 3 liðum".
3. "Hætta við" comes first, is quiet, and returns focus to the button. After merging, there's
   "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds, and the announcement says how many liðir were kept and dropped.

**Why keeping everything is the default:** it's the only option that never deletes what a leader
wrote. Its cost, a longer evening, is shown in minutes before they confirm, and shows up again
in the sheet's total afterwards, where it's easy to fix. Dropping work is still one choice away,
but it has to be chosen, and it names what goes. A modal wasn't used because the decision is
about the lanes right below it, and the leader may want to look at them while deciding.

<Canvas of={Stories.MergeKeepOne} />

**What the reducer would need:** `mergeBand` with a `keep: "all" | patrolId` argument, as
`labMerge` does here (`labBench.tsx` · `planMerge`), plus a snapshot for undo.

## 7. Recommendation

- **A · Time axis** as the desktop replacement for the current grid.
- **D · Cards** as its phone form.
- **B · Stations** as a second view of the same lanes, offered when the lanes share stations,
  since it's the only one that catches clashes.
- **C** if printing turns out to matter more than expected.
- **The merge proposal** whichever direction is picked.
