# SplitGrid: purpose and styles

SplitGrid is what a section of a fundur turns into after "Skipta á flokka": one lane per flokkur,
running at the same time. It sits inside the fundur sheet (**Lab → FundurSheet**) in place of the
section's list of liðir.

All four styles are live. Every move dispatches the real `benchReducer` intents
(`laneMoveAcross`, `laneMove`, `laneAdd`), so the announcements are the bench's own, and drag uses
the same `dragId` vocabulary and collision rule as `BenchProvider`. The lab adds only what the
merge proposal needs (`labMerge` and `labRestore` in `labBench.tsx`).

## 1. Why a leader splits a section, and what they need to see

The sveit splits up for part of the evening and comes back together: a stöðvaleikur (each
flokkur starts at a different station and rotates), different levels, flokkastarf, or one-of gear
like a single prímus. The lanes run at the same time, and the section ends when the last flokkur
is done: "Lengsti flokkur ræður."

What the leader needs, in order:

1. **Which flokkur does what, and when.** This is the plan, and it's read at the fundur from a
   phone.
2. **Whether the lanes are balanced.** The longest lane sets the section's length. A flokkur that
   finishes early is standing around, so a wait has to be visible, with a number.
3. **How to move a liður to another flokkur:** ← →, drag, or the "Færa til:" buttons, and every
   move is announced.
4. **What merging does.** Today it is silent about what it keeps.

Patrol identity is always the vivid colour **and** the name together. Colour is never the only
cue.

## 2. Why the form stays

The current form is right for the job. Time runs down the side, so the lanes are visibly
_simultaneous_. Each liður is as tall as it is long, so a short lane _looks_ short, and its free
tail is offered as "+ N mín bið" rather than left blank. Leaders already know it from the hi-fi.
What it lacks is finish:

- generic cards with a left stripe;
- a 3px top border as the only patrol cue;
- 12px uppercase footer text;
- a merge that drops lanes without saying so.

So the four variations keep the DOM, the behaviour and the copy, and change only the styling:
the axis, one column per flokkur, liðir stacked, the hint "← → færa milli flokka", the summary
line "Ristin er 75 mín — frá 19:40 til 20:55. Lengsti flokkur ræður: Uglur (75 mín). Bið: …", and
"Sameina í eitt".

**What every style shares:**

- Heights are exact (a fixed number of rem per minute), so each column lines up with the axis and
  with the others.
- The waiting lane says "+ 30 mín bið", and that wait is a button that adds a liður sized to fit.
- The column header gives the total in minutes, plus "N mín bið" or "ræður lengd".
- A tapped liður shows a strip with a named button for every other flokkur ("Færa til: Ernir,
  Uglur…"), plus ↑ Fyrr, ↓ Síðar and ✕.
- Keyboard: ← → moves a liður to the previous or next flokkur and ↑ ↓ reorders it. Focus follows
  the liður to its new column, and the reducer announces "Liður færður milli flokka: … Fyrri
  flokkur: Refir. Nýr flokkur: Ernir."
- Drag onto another column.
- Handles 2 to 6 flokkar, an empty lane ("Ekkert skráð · + Bæta við"), long titles and flokkur
  names, and an undated fundur (the axis reads "+15 mín").
- Tokens only. The patrol colour arrives as `--lane` / `--lane-on`, and it only ever sits behind
  its own `-foreground`, as an edge or a swatch, or as a 14% tint under primary text. That gives
  AA in light and dark, and axe passes in every theme.
- `countIs` for every count. Transitions only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

**On a phone** (the box narrower than 40rem): the same form, **scrolled sideways with snap**.
Two columns are in view and a sliver of the third shows, so it's clear there's more. The axis is
pinned on the left, and a line above the grid says "Strjúktu til hliðar til að sjá alla 6
flokka." A liður is 0.3rem per minute there, so a 10-minute liður is 48px tall.

Why not stacked columns: stacking would lose the one thing the form exists for, which is seeing
two flokkar side by side at the same clock. Snap keeps that, two at a time, and focus scrolls a
moved liður into view.

## 3. The styles

### S1 · Clean

<Canvas of={Stories.CleanUneven} />

**Story:** the current grid, tidied. "Refir · 70 mín · 5 mín bið" sits on a band of Refir's
blue, and below it the evening reads like a timetable.

**Why:** the patrol colour does the most work as a full header band with dark text. It's
unmistakable from a distance and always paired with the name. The liðir lose their card chrome
(border, shadow, stripe) and become plain rows: time and minutes on one line, then the title.
That halves the visual noise, so the hatched waits are what stands out.

**Trade-offs:** it's the least distinctive style. With six vivid bands side by side the header
row is loud, though it's the only loud thing on the screen.

### S2 · Stamped

<Canvas of={Stories.StampedUneven} />

**Story:** the Dagatal's picked D4 · Stimplar, carried into the fundur. Each flokkur's column is
labelled like an inked rubber stamp (a double rule in the patrol colour, uppercase, a slight
tilt), and each liður carries its status as a small stamp: **KLÁRT** pressed and tilted, **DRÖG**
dashed, **ÓVÍST** dotted amber, and **?** for óákveðið.

**Why:** it's the only style that shows status in the grid, which matters for the last check
before a fundur ("is everything klárt?"). It matches the calendar, so the plan reads as one
system. The calendar's stamp kit (now `components/plan/calendar/Stamp.tsx`) was mid-build when this was written (its CSS module didn't
exist yet), so the stamp is mirrored in CSS here. Swap in `Stamp` once the kit lands. The stamp
is decorative (`aria-hidden`), and the status is spoken in each liður's accessible name
("…, 15 mín, drög").

**Trade-offs:** the stamps take about 4rem of every row, so titles wrap sooner. Five-minute liðir
have no room for a stamp to sit beside the time.

### S3 · Patrol flags

<Canvas of={Stories.FlagsUneven} />

**Story:** each column flies its flokkur's swallowtail pennant. Under it is the total as a tally
(one mark per 5 minutes, struck in fives) and the longest lane is inked **ræður lengd**.

**Why:** a pennant is how a scout flokkur marks itself in camp, so the column reads as _ours_.
The tally makes balance something you count at a glance: three full fives against two. Liðir
are tinted in the patrol colour so the lanes stay apart without stripes.

**Trade-offs:** the most decorative style. The tally is aria-hidden; the minutes next to it are
the accessible total. At 90 minutes or more the tally gets long and wraps.

### S4 · Paper

<Canvas of={Stories.PaperUneven} />

**Story:** the plan as a printed sheet to pin up or take to the fundur. It's ruled every 5
minutes with a heavier rule every 15, the times are in typewriter face, there's a margin rule by
the axis, and ink lines frame it.

**Why:** a lot of leaders still print the evening. A sheet that already looks printed prints
well: `@media print` hides the buttons and the hint and lets the grid run full width. The ruled
lines make the minutes countable by eye. The patrol colour shrinks to a swatch and a left edge,
which survive greyscale printing because the name is always next to them.

**Trade-offs:** it's the quietest on screen, and the patrol colour is the weakest here. The
monospace times are wider, so the numbers in a narrow column crowd sooner.

## 4. "Sameina í eitt" that says what it keeps

<Canvas of={Stories.Merge} />

Today the button keeps the first flokkur's lane and drops the others in silence.

1. **Lanes still identical** (for example right after a split): nothing is lost, so it merges at
   once, with "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds (UX rule 5).
2. **Lanes that differ:** the button reads "Sameina í eitt…" and opens an inline confirmation,
   which takes focus when the leader opens it.
   - **Halda öllu, hvað á eftir öðru** (the default) keeps every distinct liður once, in the
     first flokkur's order, then whatever the others had beyond that. The preview says which
     flokkar had each liður and what it costs: "6 liðir í einni röð, 85 mín í stað 75 mín
     samhliða. Fundurinn lengist um 10 mín."
   - **Halda einni dagskrá** plus a choice of flokkur. A red list shows what goes, "Fellur út: 3
     liðir · 40 mín", and the button turns red: "Sameina og sleppa 3 liðum".
3. "Hætta við" comes first, is quiet, and returns focus to the button. After merging,
   "Afturkalla" stays for 10 seconds, and the announcement says how many liðir were kept and
   dropped.

**Why keep everything by default:** it's the only choice that never deletes what a leader wrote.
Its cost, a longer evening, is shown in minutes before they confirm, and shows up again in the
sheet's total afterwards. To ship it, `mergeBand` needs a `keep: "all" | patrolId` argument, as
`labMerge` has here.
