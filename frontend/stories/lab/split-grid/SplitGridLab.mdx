# SplitGrid: bank cards you can edit

SplitGrid is what a section of a fundur turns into after "Skipta á flokka": one lane per flokkur,
running at the same time. It sits inside the fundur sheet (**Lab → FundurSheet**) in place of the
section's list of liðir.

This row replaced the earlier style variations (S1–S4, scrapped 2026-10-20). The idea: **each
liður is the bank card it came from**, the same card a leader dragged out of BlockPalette, and
that card is where the leader changes it for one flokkur. The time grid, the merge proposal and
every move path stay as they were.

**K2 · Flip it over was picked** (2026-10-20) from four ways of editing a card; K1 (on the card),
K3 (editor panel) and K4 (follows the sveit) are removed. K2 is now being polished: the bugs found
and fixed are in section 5.

Everything is live. Moves and edits dispatch the real `benchReducer` intents (`laneMoveAcross`,
`laneMove`, `laneAdd`, `laneRemove`, `lanePatch`), so the announcements are the bench's own, and
drag uses the same `dragId` vocabulary as `BenchProvider` (where a card lands is the lab's own,
section 6). The lab adds only what the merge proposal and Afturkalla need (`labMerge` and
`labRestore` in `labBench.tsx`).

## 1. Why a leader splits a section, and what they need to see

The sveit splits up for part of the evening and comes back together: a stöðvaleikur (each
flokkur starts at a different station and rotates), different levels, flokkastarf, or one-of gear
like a single prímus. The lanes run at the same time, and the section ends when the last flokkur
is done: "Lengsti flokkur ræður."

What the leader needs, in order:

1. **Which flokkur does what, and when.** This is the plan, and it's read at the fundur from a
   phone.
2. **Whether the lanes are balanced.** The longest lane sets the section's length. A flokkur that
   finishes early is standing around, so a wait has to be visible, with a number.
3. **How to move a liður:** up and down its flokkur's timeline, or to a place in another
   flokkur. Drag, ↑ ↓ ← →, or the "Færa til:" buttons, and every move is announced.
4. **What this flokkur does differently.** A station's leader, a shorter version for the
   youngest, the búnaður they bring, or another block altogether, without losing sight of what
   the rest of the sveit does.
5. **What merging does.** Today it is silent about what it keeps.

Patrol identity is always the vivid colour **and** the name together. Colour is never the only
cue.

## 2. Why bank cards

The current lanes draw a liður as a text block: a time, the minutes and a title. Every liður
looks the same, so a leader reads every word to find the station they're looking for, and
there is nothing to change but the order and the flokkur.

A block already has a face in the bank: its picture (its own image, or its theme's icon on its
kind's tint), its name, its kind and theme. Using that same card on the plan:

- **Recognition instead of reading.** The leader picked "Hnútar og bönd" by its rope picture in
  the bank; the same picture finds it in Uglur's column at 20:10.
- **One object, one look.** A block is the same thing in the bank, on the bench and in a split
  lane. BlockPalette and this lab draw the picture tile and kind line from the same component
  (`components/plan/bench/BlockCard.tsx`), so they can't drift apart.
- **Room for what the flokkur made of it.** Who leads it, and a note, on the card itself.

What stays from the current form: time down the side, one column per flokkur, each closed card
exactly as tall as it is long (0.35rem a minute, so 15 minutes is 84px, room for the picture and
two lines), the free tail as "+ N mín bið", the column header's total and "ræður lengd", the
summary line, ← → ↑ ↓, drag, and the merge proposal. On a phone the columns scroll sideways with
snap, two in view, the axis pinned.

**The card, top to bottom:** the picture; a clock icon with the length ("15 mín", sharp in body
ink) and the clock span after it, quieter (left out in a column narrower than 13rem, where the
axis carries the time); the name; the kind and theme (from 20 minutes up); who leads it and the
adaptation note. The name has two lines at most, and one under 20 minutes. Under 10 minutes the
card is a single line: the length and the name, no picture. Its status is the calendar's stamp in
the corner (the same rubber-stamp language as the Dagatal, PlanGrid A5 and FundurSheet F2), and
an óákveðið card has a dashed edge. No coloured stripe: the column's flokkur name carries the
patrol colour, and the picture carries the kind.

## 3. What a card can change, and the sveit's version

The back of the card offers these edits, all applied at once (no Save button to forget):

- **Lengd:** − and + in steps of 5, never under 5 minutes. The column's total and the band's
  length follow at once.
- **Staða:** Staðfest, Drög, Óvíst, Óákveðið, as a radio group. The stamp changes with it.
- **Hver stýrir:** a name.
- **Aðlögun fyrir flokkinn:** what this flokkur does differently ("Förum út að Elliðaánum.").
- **Búnaður:** starts as the bank block's own list, add with Enter, remove with ✕.
- **Skipta út fyrir aðra blokk:** a native select of bank blocks (the phone's own picker). The
  card takes the new block's picture, kind, length and búnaður; the adaptation note is cleared
  because it was about the old block.
- **Færa til:** a named button for every other flokkur, then ↑ Fyrr, ↓ Síðar.
- **Fjarlægja úr flokknum:** the only step with an Afturkalla (10 seconds, UX rule 5). Focus
  moves to Afturkalla.

**The sveit's version.** After a split every flokkur has a copy of the same liður. When one
flokkur's copy changes, the leader needs to see that it now differs, from what, and how to put
it back. So each card knows its _útgáfa sveitarinnar_: a changed card is marked with a dot by its stamp,
and its back lists the differences ("Stýrir: Óli (sveitin: Sigga)") with
**Nota útgáfu sveitarinnar**. A liður only one flokkur has has nothing to differ from.

## 4. K2 · Flip it over

<Canvas of={Stories.Open} />

**Story:** "The back of the card is where you write." Pressing a card turns it over (a quarter
turn, 240ms, none under reduced motion). Its picture and name stay at the top of the back, so it
is still the same card, and the back holds the whole editor on a plain surface.

**Hierarchy:** the card's identity (picture, flokkur, name, "Lokið"), then what differs from the
sveit, then the fields in the order a leader thinks of them, then the moves, then Fjarlægja.

**Why:** a clean split between reading (front) and changing (back). The front never carries
controls, so the grid stays calm.

**How it behaves:**

- The back is **lifted above the grid**, placed over its own card: at least 24rem wide, moved
  left when it would run off the right edge, the full width on a phone. Nothing in the grid
  moves: every column keeps its width, every card its height, and the time axis stays true.
- The box grows underneath a tall back, so the back never covers the summary or Afturkalla.
- The front stays in the grid under it and changes at once (length, stamp, the changed dot).
- Pressing the card again, "Lokið" or Escape turns it back and gives focus to the card. Focus
  moves to the back's heading when it opens. Moving an open card ("Færa til", Fyrr, Síðar) keeps
  it open, and focus stays on its back.

**Trade-offs:** the back covers part of the neighbouring columns while it's open, so two cards
can't be compared then. The turn is a gimmick if it's slow, so it is short and only when motion
is welcome.

## 5. Polishing K2: the bugs found and fixed

Found in screenshots of every K2 story (front and back, short and long cards, light and dark,
desktop and phone, at 2× scale):

1. **The open column widened and squeezed the others.** Its column went to `2fr` / 17rem, so
   every other column shrank the moment a card opened. _Fixed:_ every column keeps its width;
   the back is lifted over the grid instead.
2. **The time axis lied while a card was open.** The open card pushed the rest of its column
   down, so that column's cards no longer lined up with the axis or with the other columns.
   _Fixed:_ the card's slot keeps its height; the grid never moves.
3. **The grid's lines showed through the back.** The back was "ruled like a playing card", and
   the lines ran through the fields and labels like the grid's own rules bleeding through.
   _Fixed:_ a plain surface.
4. **Text left blurred after the turn.** The turn's animation kept its final 3D transform
   (`fill-mode: both`), which keeps the back on its own composited layer and can blur its text.
   _Fixed:_ `backwards` fill, so nothing is left on the back once it has turned.
5. **The length was a blob over the picture.** "15" sat in a white chip laid over the picture's
   corner, hard to read on the light tiles and covering the picture. _Fixed:_ a clock icon and
   "15 mín" in body ink, on the card's first line with the clock span (and the same in
   BlockPalette, through the shared `BlockMinutes`).
6. **Short cards cut their text in half.** On a 10-minute card a two-line title ("Eldfæri og
   prímus") was clipped through its second line, and on a 15-minute card the leader's line was
   cut at the bottom edge. _Fixed:_ the name has one line under 20 minutes and two at most
   otherwise (the full name is in the card's title and accessible name); under 20 minutes the
   kind line goes, since the picture carries the kind.
7. **A 5-minute card cut its picture in half.** 28px is too short for the 40px tile. _Fixed:_
   under 10 minutes a card is one line, the length and the name.
8. **Clock spans cut to "19:4…" in narrow columns** (six flokkar, phone). _Fixed:_ below 13rem
   the span is left out; the axis carries the time, and the card's accessible name still says
   it.
9. **On a narrow card the "changed" dot sat on the length** ("15 mí●"). _Fixed:_ below 13rem the
   dot moves to the card's bottom corner.

## 6. Dragging along the timeline and between flokkar

A card can be dragged up and down its own column, to change the order and so the clock, or to a
chosen place in another flokkur's column: not just "to the end of that flokkur".

- **The grid shows the result while you drag.** The card is taken out of its column and put in
  at the spot under the pointer, so the clock times, the column totals, "ræður lengd" and
  "+ N mín bið" are the ones the drop will give. The spot it would land in is a dashed outline in
  its own height, with a line along its top edge and "hefst 19:55"; the card in the hand shows
  the clock it would get too, so the time stays readable when the hand covers the outline. The
  cards it passes slide to their new place (none of that under reduced motion).
- **Where it lands** is the column under the pointer and, in it, the number of cards whose middle
  is above the pointer (`cardDrag.ts`). Taken from the screen rather than from dnd-kit's
  collision rules, because the columns are the flokkar and their cards change height as the
  preview moves. Within 40px above or below a column still counts; anywhere else is outside.
- **Putting it back:** letting go outside the flokkar, or Escape at any point, leaves everything
  as it was and says so ("Hætt við. Skyndihjálp er á sínum stað.").
- **An open card closes when it is picked up.** Its back is lifted over the neighbouring
  columns, so it can't travel with the card without covering where it is going; the card goes
  as its front, and the leader opens it again where it landed. (The other choice, dragging only
  from the front, would leave an open card with no way to move it but the buttons.)
- **Afturkalla** follows every drop, for 10 seconds. The arrow keys move one step at a time and
  the opposite arrow undoes it, so they don't get one.
- **On a phone** a swipe across the cards scrolls the page and the columns, as everywhere else;
  a finger that holds still on a card for 200ms picks it up, and from then on the page stays
  still under it. There is no separate handle: the whole card is the handle (a 10-minute card is
  still 3.5rem tall). Mouse and touch are separate sensors, because the pointer sensor would also
  take a touch after 5px of a swipe. Before this the card had `touch-action: none`, so a
  swipe that started on a card couldn't scroll at all.
- **Keyboard:** ↑ ↓ change the order and ← → change the flokkur, exactly as a drop does, and the
  reducer announces each step. **Screen readers** hear the drag too: "Liður tekinn upp:
  Skyndihjálp, 15 mín…", then each new spot ("Ernir: 2. af 4, hefst 19:55."), then the move or
  the cancel. Flokkur names are never declined after a preposition; they stand before a colon.

<Canvas of={Stories.Drag} />

_Trade-off:_ the lifted card sits under the finger, so on a phone the outline it is aiming at can
be partly covered; the card's own clock says where it would start. The real bench
(`BenchProvider`) still uses the pointer sensor and `touch-action: none`; if this is promoted,
it should take the same sensors.

## 7. The flokkur at the top of its column

The pill was the one generic thing left on the grid: every flokkur read as the same lozenge in a
different colour. A first round tried four heads (pennant, crest, stamp, colour band). The pick
combined **H2's crest** (the flokkur's animal in a round badge) with **H4's rule** (a line in the
flokkur's colour down its column), refined as H5a–d (pole, lanyard, time rule, tinted lane). The
final head is **H6: H5b's lanyard on H5d's tinted lane**, and it is K2's default.

<Canvas of={Stories.Headers} />

**What H6 says.** The crest says _whose_ column this is, with some character; the cord carries
that down the column's edge, and the wash makes the whole column the flokkur's, so a card at 20:40
still reads as Ernir's without looking back up. Hierarchy: crest and name first, then the total
and the wait; the cord and the wash are the quietest, colour around the cards rather than on them.

**How it is drawn.**

- **The crest** is a ring: the flokkur's colour around a centre of paper, the animal in body ink.
  The paper centre keeps it standing off the wash in both themes (H5b's pale `-subtle` centre is
  nearly the wash's own colour, so it would blend in).
- **The cord** is 2px. It drops from the bottom of the ring, turns with a soft corner a little
  above the head's foot, and runs down the column's left edge to the bottom of the grid. The head
  draws it to its foot; the column carries it on (`.lane::before`) at the same x and width, so
  there is no step. Cards and the wait sit to its right, so it never runs through text.
- **The wash** is the patrol's own colour at 10% (`hsl(var(--lane) / 0.1)`), behind the head and
  the whole column, under the 15-minute lines. One formula for both themes: the dark `-subtle`
  step (-900) was tried and is a saturated block of colour, not a wash. Secondary text, the totals
  and "+ N mín bið" keep AA on it (the a11y tests check contrast in light and dark).
- **The name** is a step larger (body-lg, the heading font), next to the crest; the total and
  "ræður lengd" / "N mín bið" sit under it.

**With dragging and the open card.** The drop column's highlight (the primary outline and tint)
replaces the wash while a card is over it, so the target is unmistakable; the dashed landing slot
and the lifted card paint over the cord. The open K2 back is a plain surface with a shadow, lifted
over the grid, so it reads the same over a neighbour's wash as over the page.

**Trade-offs.** Four to six tinted columns put more colour on the screen than the pill did; the
10% wash is the point where the lanes still read as separate without competing with the cards. In
a column too narrow for the name beside the crest (six flokkar, a phone), the turning cord would
cross the name, so there it hangs straight from the edge and the name goes under the crest. The
yellow cord on its own wash is the faintest pair; that is fine because the cord never carries
information on its own (the name and numbers are text).

**What was tried and dropped.** A cord down the middle of the column behind the cards (it only
showed as stitches between cards); a time rule whose length was the flokkur's minutes (H5c: the
most information, but one more thing to learn); a filled crest on a 3px pole (H5a: plainer than the
lanyard); the wash without the cord (H5d: the lane read as a block, not as something hanging from
the flokkur's badge).

**The crests.** lucide has no fox, owl or bear, so the animals are drawn for Slóði in
`crestMarks.tsx`: refur, örn (also fálki), ugla, björn, úlfur, hrafn and selur. They follow
lucide's grid (24 units, a 2px stroke, round caps and joins) so they sit with its icons, and they
are a few strokes each so they read at 28–36px. One table maps a word in the name to a mark, first
match wins. A few invented names that aren't animals borrow a lucide icon (Þrumuskýin a storm
cloud, geimverur a rocket, ofurhetjur a bolt); anything else gets its initials ("FF", "H").

### Long names

Leaders name flokkar whatever they like: "Fjólublá fljúgandi avókadó svínin", "Ofurhetjur úr
Breiðholti", "Litlu grænu geimverurnar frá Mars", or one compound with nowhere to break,
"Hraðbrautarbrúarsmiðirnir". Every head treats them the same way on purpose:

- **Two lines at most.** The name wraps; `hyphens: auto` with `lang="is"` on the band hyphenates
  where the browser has an Icelandic dictionary, and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` breaks a compound
  that still doesn't fit. Past two lines it ends in "…".
- **The whole name is never lost.** The clamp is visual only: the heading's text is the full name
  (a screen reader hears all of it) and it is the tooltip.
- **Nothing overlaps.** The numbers always sit under the name, never beside it, so a long name
  pushes them down rather than running into them; all heads in a row grow together.
- **In H6:** the crest always stays; in a column too narrow for both (six flokkar, a phone) the
  name goes under the crest, a step smaller, and a name with no picture shows its initials in the
  badge. The cord hangs straight from the edge there, so it never crosses the name. (The old pill
  became a rounded block of two lines.)
- **The cards in a phone column.** The length is the one thing a card never cuts: "15 mín" stays
  whole, the clock span goes first, and in a column under 9.5rem the picture goes too, so the
  length and the title get the card's whole width. A long leader or note ends in "…" on one line
  (its icon used to wrap onto a line the card had no room for).
- **The flip side** names other flokkar on its "Færa til" buttons: they wrap to two lines and clamp
  the same way, with the full name as tooltip and in the accessible name, "Færa Kakó og spjall í
  annan flokk: Fjólublá fljúgandi avókadó svínin" (the name after a colon, never declined after
  "til"). The editor's own heading clamps the flokkur's name the same way.

_Found while testing:_ Chromium on Linux has no Icelandic hyphenation dictionary, so there the

rule, or a hyphenation library.

## 8. "Sameina í eitt" that says what it keeps

<Canvas of={Stories.Merge} />

Unchanged from the earlier style lab. Today the button keeps the first flokkur's lane and drops
the others in silence.

1. **Lanes still identical** (for example right after a split): nothing is lost, so it merges at
   once, with "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds (UX rule 5).
2. **Lanes that differ:** the button reads "Sameina í eitt…" and opens an inline confirmation,
   which takes focus when the leader opens it.
   - **Halda öllu, hvað á eftir öðru** (the default) keeps every distinct liður once, in the
     first flokkur's order, then whatever the others had beyond that. The preview says which
     flokkar had each liður and what it costs: "6 liðir í einni röð, 85 mín í stað 75 mín
     samhliða. Fundurinn lengist um 10 mín."
   - **Halda einni dagskrá** plus a choice of flokkur. A red list shows what goes, "Fellur út: 3
     liðir · 40 mín", and the button turns red: "Sameina og sleppa 3 liðum".
3. "Hætta við" comes first, is quiet, and returns focus to the button. After merging,
   "Afturkalla" stays for 10 seconds, and the announcement says how many liðir were kept and
   dropped.

**Why keep everything by default:** it's the only choice that never deletes what a leader wrote.
Its cost, a longer evening, is shown in minutes before they confirm, and shows up again in the
sheet's total afterwards. To ship it, `mergeBand` needs a `keep: "all" | patrolId` argument, as
`labMerge` has here.

## 9. On a phone: stacked by time (M3, M3a–d)

Desktop K2 with H6 is approved. On a phone, the same grid is squeezed into 390px and scrolled
sideways. The layouts below take over when the band itself is 620px wide or less. That is
measured on the band, so a narrow bench pane counts as a phone too. Pass them with the `phone`
prop (`"m3"`, `"m3a"` … `"m3d"`); without it, a narrow band stays as it is today. Wider than a
phone, it is always the desktop grid.

Of the four phone layouts tried first (one flokkur at a time, compare two, stacked by time,
list per flokkur), **M3 · Stacked by time** was picked for its function. M1, M2 and M4 are gone.
What follows is M3 as it was, then four styles of the same function.

### What gets squished today (390×844 and 360×740)

Screenshots with 2, 4 and 6 flokkar, short and long names, an open card and a drag:

1. **One and a half flokkar in view.** Columns are 136px (8.5rem, their floor) at both widths.
   The second column is cut through its cards ("Áttaviti og k", "Hnútar og bö"), so you can't compare
   two flokkar. With six flokkar, four are off screen with nothing to say which.
2. **Titles end in "…" in both columns.** In a 15-minute card the title gets one line
   ("Eldfæri og…") and the leader line, because the card is only 136px wide.
3. **The column head is a quarter of the screen.** The crest, a two-line name and the numbers
   take about 110px before the first card. The second column's numbers run off the edge ("70
   mín ræðu", "45 mín 30 mín bið").
4. **The pinned time axis scrolled away.** Past the first screen of columns, the axis left with
   the grid, because the grid was only as wide as the scroller. Sticky stays inside its grid, so
   at the last flokkur there was no time on the left at all. A timeline has no sideways grid,
   so it can't happen there.
5. **The open card covers everything.** Its back is the full width and about 900px tall, more
   than a screen, laid over the grid. You lose which column and which time you are editing.
   "Færa til" and Fyrr / Síðar wrap onto a second row.
6. **No sense of the whole.** "Lengsti flokkur ræður" and each flokkur's wait are only in the
   summary sentence under the grid, one screen down.
7. **The wait boxes are large dead areas.** "+ 30 mín bið" is a hatched block the height of 30
   minutes in a column you can only half see.
8. **Help text for a mouse.** The foot says "Smelltu" and lists arrow keys, and is three lines
   long.
9. **A touch drag lost its way across flokkar** (found while building these). While dragged, a
   card is redrawn in the flokkur under the finger. A touch keeps going to the element it
   started on, even after that element leaves the page, and from then on its moves no longer
   bubble up to the window. The band stopped hearing the finger after the first new flokkur.
   `useFollowPointer` now also listens on the touched element, which fixes it in every layout,
   desktop K2 on a touch screen included.

### What every look keeps (the M3 function)

- **One timeline down the page.** At each time something starts, what every flokkur starts
  then. Where a flokkur is done before the band, its wait, with "+ Bæta við", which adds a
  liður that fits the wait. "Lok · allir koma saman" at the band's end.
- **A legend of the flokkar** at the top: crest, name, total, and "ræður lengd" or the wait.
- **K2's card, opened as a sheet from the bottom** (`PhoneSheet`). A tap on a liður opens its
  card's back. Fyrr / Síðar move it in time, "Færa til" moves it to another flokkur, and remove
  comes with Afturkalla. It is a native modal `<dialog>`: focus stays in it, the page behind is
  inert, and Escape, Lokið or a tap on the dimmed page closes it, with focus back on the liður.
  It is at most 85% of the screen tall and clear of the home bar.
- **No dragging.** The rows are times, not places; a long press does nothing, so the page
  scrolls freely under a thumb.
- **H6's identity.** The ringed crest and the flokkur's colour as a cord or edge, with its 10%
  wash under primary text, never coloured text on the page.
- **Everything the band does.** Split and the honest merge, Afturkalla, every edit, ↑ ↓ ← → on
  a keyboard, and the announcements are the band's own and unchanged.
- **48px targets** (`--sl-touch-target`), and the hint at the foot is written for a thumb.
- **Screen readers hear a timeline.** An ordered list "Tímalína" in time order. Every liður's
  name says its flokkur, time, name, length, status and end ("Refir kl. 20:10: Áttaviti og
  kort, 15 mín, staðfest, til 20:25"), and every time says who is still busy ("Heldur áfram:
  Uglur: Ratleikur um hverfið til 20:40"), so the crests, cords and tracks are decoration.

### Lengths that don't line up

A stöðvaleikur of equal stations is the easy case: everyone changes at once. Once a leader
plans per flokkur, lengths differ: Refir do a 30-minute liður and then two of 15, Ernir three
of 20, Uglur one Ratleikur of 60, Birnir 10 and then 40. Starts no longer line up, and a long
liður runs past the others' starts. The first versions listed starts only, so at 20:00 you saw
Ernir start Merkjamál and nothing about the three others. A leader glancing at 20:00 needs to
see that Refir are 20 minutes into their 30, Ernir start their second liður, Uglur are still on
the Ratleikur, and Birnir 10 minutes into their 40.

So every look now shows, at every time:

1. **each liður's span**: its length and "til 20:10" beside it;
2. **who is still busy** with a liður that started earlier, and until when ("heldur áfram");
3. **where each flokkur ends** and how long it waits, with "+ Bæta við".

The phone stories use that case (`mixed`: Refir 30 + 15 + 15, Ernir 20 × 3, Uglur 60, Birnir
10 + 40 and a 10-minute wait) and a six-flokkar version with lengths from 5 to 60 (`mixedSix`,
adding Selir 5 + 25 + 15 + 10 and Hrafnar 45 + 15). Each look below says how it shows it.

### How long they get

The band's timeline area, in px, measured in the stories (legend included; the band's own head
and foot left out). The four and six flokkar are the mixed lengths; the long names are a
stöðvaleikur of 15-minute stations.

| M3a · Rail (as it opens) | 732                    | 976                    | 636                 | 784                 |

M3 is short here because it says nothing about who is still busy. Showing it costs M3b and
M3d one quiet line per time (about 30px); M3a pays only in its open stops. M3c got shorter,
not longer: a card spans its rows instead of a "heldur áfram" placeholder taking a place in
each, and with four flokkar side by side a time is one row, not two.

### M3a · Rail

<Canvas of={Stories.PhoneM3a} />

**The story it tells:** "the evening is a line of stops; open the one you're working on." A
leader planning a split thinks one station change at a time.

**Hierarchy:** the legend, then a rail down the left with the times beside it in mono and a node
at every start time. Each stop folds to one 48px line: the crests of the flokkar that start
there, overlapping like a hand of cards, then what starts ("Tjaldreising") and the count ("1
byrjar · 3 halda áfram · 1 bíður"). Open, it lists every flokkur: who starts, as M3's card lines
with "til 20:30" beside the length; who is still busy, as a slim line in the flokkur's wash
("Uglur · Ratleikur um hverfið", "heldur áfram til 20:40 · 20 af 60 mín") with a bar along its
foot for how far in it is; and who waits. An open stop's
node is filled, a folded one is a ring. "Opna allt" opens every stop.

**Why:** it's the only look that stays short however many flokkar there are: about 50px a stop
folded, so six flokkar' 60 minutes is 832px with the first stop open. The folded line still says
who is busy and with what, and the rail gives the evening a shape.

**Rules:** the first stop is open to begin with. The stop of the card in the sheet is always
open, so a Fyrr or Síðar that carries it to another time never hides it.

**Concurrency:** the most explicit of the four. An open stop is a full answer to "what is
everyone doing at 20:00", in words and with a progress bar, and the folded count says how many
are mid-liður. The cost is length when open: every stop repeats every busy flokkur, so six
flokkar with "Opna allt" are long.

**Trade-offs:** a folded stop hides the lengths and stamps, so "is everything confirmed?" needs
"Opna allt". The crests overlap: with six, the initials of one ("FF") are partly covered by the
next. One more tap than the other looks before a liður can be opened.

**Structure:** the list "Tímalína"; each stop's head is a disclosure button (`aria-expanded`,
`aria-controls`) and its liðir a list.

### M3b · Ruled log

<Canvas of={Stories.PhoneM3b} />

**The story it tells:** "the run sheet a foringi holds at the fundur": times down a margin, a
ruled line per thing that happens, nothing decorative.

**Hierarchy:** a heavy rule under the legend, then the times in a mono margin, written once per
time with a rule between times. Each start is one ruled line, exactly 48px: the crest (small),
the name, "15 mín til 20:25" at the right, the stamp. A wait is a line on the flokkur's wash
("10 mín bið +").

**Concurrency: tracks.** Between the time and the lines is a gutter with a narrow track per
flokkur, in flokkur order, running unbroken from time to time. A liður is a solid segment of its
flokkur's colour for as long as it lasts; a new liður breaks the track with a gap of paper; a
wait is dashed. So at a glance Uglur's track is one long segment beside Ernir's three, and
where a track goes dashed that flokkur is waiting. Under each time's lines one quiet line says
who is still busy and until when ("Heldur áfram Refir til 20:10 · Uglur til 20:40"); a reader
hears the liðir's names too. The rows aren't proportional to time (a 5-minute gap and a
30-minute one are the same height); the "til" times and the tracks carry the lengths.

**Shared lines:** flokkar starting the same liður at the same time (the same name, length and
status), as right after "Skipta á flokka" or at a station two flokkar share, get one line. The
name is said once, then one 48px crest button per flokkur, each opening that flokkur's card. In
the uneven fixture Ernir and Uglur both start Eldfæri og prímus at 19:55 and share a line.

**Why:** the densest line per start (48px, no gaps), calm and easy to scan down the margin. It
prints well and matches FundurSheet's F3 · Run sheet.

**Trade-offs:** a shared line is tapped on a crest, not on the line, which is a second way of
opening a card. A single line names the liður but not the flokkur on screen, only the crest
(the legend and the accessible name say it). With five or six busy, the "heldur áfram" line is
clamped to two lines and ends in "…"; the tracks still show everyone. At 360 with "til 20:25"
beside the length, titles get about 90px and end in "…" ("Hnútar og …").

### M3c · Cards in rows

<Canvas of={Stories.PhoneM3c} />

**The story it tells:** "the flokkar side by side, and time runs down": the bank cards, as tall
as their liðir.

**Hierarchy:** the legend, then a column per flokkur under its crest and name (pinned while the
column scrolls by), and time running down with every start time in a mono margin and a hairline
across. A card is as tall as its liður: it spans the rows from its start to its end, at 0.3rem a
minute (at least 1.75rem a row, more if a card needs it). Its flokkur's colour is its top edge;
inside, the name (three lines), the clock and length, then "til 20:10" and the stamp. A wait
card ("10 mín bið + Bæta við", dashed, on the wash) fills from where the flokkur is done to Lok.

**Concurrency:** the most literal. Read across at any height and you see what every flokkur is
doing: Uglur's 60-minute Ratleikur is one tall card beside Ernir's three 20-minute ones, and
Refir's 30 is half its height. Nothing needs saying "heldur áfram" on screen; a reader hears it
at each time.

**Five or six flokkar** are two timelines of three, one under the other (Refir, Ernir, Uglur,
then Birnir, Selir, Hrafnar), each with its own times. Six columns on a 360px phone leave a card
about 50px, the squeeze this whole section is about; three leave about 90px.

**Why:** time as height is how the desktop grid reads, and here it fits a phone because the
cards are narrow and the columns few. It is the shortest look with four flokkar (532px).

**Trade-offs:** four columns at 390px leave a card about 70px, enough for "Tjaldreising" (66px)
but not for a long compound, which breaks mid-word (no Icelandic hyphenation in this browser).
The margin and gaps are tight for that. Rows grow when a short liður's card needs more room
than its minutes, so heights are only roughly proportional. With six flokkar the two halves
can't be compared side by side.

**Structure:** each grid is an ordered list "Tímalína" in time order: at each start time its
time (with who is still busy, for a reader), then the cards that start then.

### M3d · Threads

<Canvas of={Stories.PhoneM3d} />

**The story it tells:** "the sveit splits, every flokkur goes its own way, and they all meet
again at Lok."

**Hierarchy:** the legend, then the times in a mono margin and a cord per flokkur, 12px apart,
in flokkur order. At the top the cords leave one bar ("Sveitin skiptist"); at Lok they join it
again. Each start is a line: a bead on its own flokkur's cord, the name, "15 mín" and the
flokkur, the stamp. While a flokkur waits for the others, its cord is thin and dashed and its
wait line has a hollow bead ("10 mín bið + Bæta við").

**Concurrency:** a cord is thick and solid for as long as a liður lasts, and its bead marks where
the next one starts, so Uglur's cord is one bead and a long run beside Ernir's three beads. The
room under a time grows a little with the minutes until the next (a 30-minute gap is taller than
a 5-minute one), so the page keeps some of the clock's shape. After each time's starts, one
quiet line with the cords running past says who is still busy and until when.

**Why:** the shape of the split is visible without reading: which cord carries each line, where
the dashes start (who waits, and how long), and that it all comes back together. It is the most
personal of the four and fits H6's cords.

**Trade-offs:** the beads are colour first; the flokkur's name is on every line so it doesn't rely
on colour alone. Six cords take 78px of a 360px screen. One line per start plus a "heldur
áfram" line per time makes it the longest with six flokkar (1398px).

### Testing it

Each look has a plain story (390 and 360, four and six flokkar, long names), light and dark,
with no play test, so it shows the look as it opens. Its "… · interactions" story runs the play
test on one 390px frame of the mixed lengths:

- the timeline's times are in order, from 19:40 to "20:40 Lok";
- at 20:00 it says Uglur are still on Ratleikur um hverfið until 20:40 and Refir on Hnútar og
  bönd until 20:10, and Uglur's card is named "…, 60 mín, …, til 20:40" (M3a–d);
- a tap opens the card as a sheet, and Lokið goes back to the liður;
- Fyrr moves Refir's Áttaviti og kort to 19:40 and Síðar back to 20:10;
- Færa til moves it to Ernir, and **Afturkalla** puts it back (a move from the card's back
  now gets Afturkalla, as a drop does, on desktop too);
- remove from the sheet, then Afturkalla;
- "+ Bæta við" fills Birnir's 10-minute wait.

M3a's test also checks that only the first stop starts open. The shared line in M3b (two
flokkar starting the same liður at the same time) isn't in the mixed fixture, so it's no longer
tested. They run on light phone, dark phone and light
desktop (the desktop stories unchanged), with a11y.
