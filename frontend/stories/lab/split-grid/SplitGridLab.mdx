# SplitGrid: bank cards you can edit

SplitGrid is what a section of a fundur turns into after "Skipta á flokka": one lane per flokkur,
running at the same time. It sits inside the fundur sheet (**Lab → FundurSheet**) in place of the
section's list of liðir.

This row (K1–K4) replaces the earlier style variations (S1–S4, scrapped 2026-10-20). The idea:
**each liður is the bank card it came from**, the same card a leader dragged out of BlockPalette,
and that card is where the leader changes it for one flokkur. The time grid, the merge proposal
and every move path stay as they were.

Everything is live. Moves and edits dispatch the real `benchReducer` intents (`laneMoveAcross`,
`laneMove`, `laneAdd`, `laneRemove`, `lanePatch`), so the announcements are the bench's own, and
drag uses the same `dragId` vocabulary and collision rule as `BenchProvider`. The lab adds only
what the merge proposal and Afturkalla need (`labMerge` and `labRestore` in `labBench.tsx`).

## 1. Why a leader splits a section, and what they need to see

The sveit splits up for part of the evening and comes back together: a stöðvaleikur (each
flokkur starts at a different station and rotates), different levels, flokkastarf, or one-of gear
like a single prímus. The lanes run at the same time, and the section ends when the last flokkur
is done: "Lengsti flokkur ræður."

What the leader needs, in order:

1. **Which flokkur does what, and when.** This is the plan, and it's read at the fundur from a
   phone.
2. **Whether the lanes are balanced.** The longest lane sets the section's length. A flokkur that
   finishes early is standing around, so a wait has to be visible, with a number.
3. **How to move a liður to another flokkur:** ← →, drag, or the "Færa til:" buttons, and every
   move is announced.
4. **What this flokkur does differently.** A station's leader, a shorter version for the
   youngest, the búnaður they bring, or another block altogether, without losing sight of what
   the rest of the sveit does.
5. **What merging does.** Today it is silent about what it keeps.

Patrol identity is always the vivid colour **and** the name together. Colour is never the only
cue.

## 2. Why bank cards

The current lanes draw a liður as a text block: a time, the minutes and a title. Every liður
looks the same, so a leader reads every word to find the station they're looking for, and
there is nothing to change but the order and the flokkur.

A block already has a face in the bank: its picture (its own image, or its theme's icon on its
kind's tint), its name, its kind and theme. Using that same card on the plan:

- **Recognition instead of reading.** The leader picked "Hnútar og bönd" by its rope picture in
  the bank; the same picture finds it in Uglur's column at 20:10.
- **One object, one look.** A block is the same thing in the bank, on the bench and in a split
  lane. BlockPalette and this lab draw the picture tile and kind line from the same component
  (`components/plan/bench/BlockCard.tsx`), so they can't drift apart.
- **Room for what the flokkur made of it.** Who leads it, and a note, on the card itself.

What stays from the current form: time down the side, one column per flokkur, each closed card
exactly as tall as it is long (0.35rem a minute, so 15 minutes is 84px, room for the picture and
two lines), the free tail as "+ N mín bið", the column header's total and "ræður lengd", the
summary line, ← → ↑ ↓, drag, and the merge proposal. On a phone the columns scroll sideways with
snap, two in view, the axis pinned.

**The card, top to bottom:** the picture with the minutes on it (like a video's length on its
thumbnail), the clock span, the name, the kind and theme (the theme drops in a column narrower
than 10rem), who leads it and the adaptation note. Its status is the calendar's stamp in the
corner (the same rubber-stamp language as the Dagatal, PlanGrid A5 and FundurSheet F2), and an
óákveðið card has a dashed edge. No coloured stripe: the column's flokkur name carries the
patrol colour, and the picture carries the kind.

## 3. What a card can change, and the sveit's version

Every direction offers the same edits, all applied at once (no Save button to forget):

- **Lengd:** − and + in steps of 5, never under 5 minutes. The column's total and the band's
  length follow at once.
- **Staða:** Staðfest, Drög, Óvíst, Óákveðið, as a radio group. The stamp changes with it.
- **Hver stýrir:** a name.
- **Aðlögun fyrir flokkinn:** what this flokkur does differently ("Förum út að Elliðaánum.").
- **Búnaður:** starts as the bank block's own list, add with Enter, remove with ✕.
- **Skipta út fyrir aðra blokk:** a native select of bank blocks (the phone's own picker). The
  card takes the new block's picture, kind, length and búnaður; the adaptation note is cleared
  because it was about the old block.
- **Færa til:** a named button for every other flokkur, then ↑ Fyrr, ↓ Síðar.
- **Fjarlægja úr flokknum:** the only step with an Afturkalla (10 seconds, UX rule 5). Focus
  moves to Afturkalla.

**The sveit's version.** After a split every flokkur has a copy of the same liður. When one
flokkur's copy changes, the leader needs to see that it now differs, from what, and how to put
it back. So each card knows its _útgáfa sveitarinnar_: a changed card is marked (a dot in K1–K3,
"BREYTT" in K4), and its editor lists the differences ("Stýrir: Óli (sveitin: Sigga)") with
**Nota útgáfu sveitarinnar**. A liður only one flokkur has is "Aðeins hér": there is nothing to
differ from.

## 4. The directions: how a card is edited

They differ in _where_ the editor appears, not in what it does, so a leader who learns one has
learned them all. Pressing a card opens it; pressing it again, "Lokið" or Escape closes it and
gives focus back to the card. Moving a closed card with ← → leaves it closed.

### K1 · On the card

<Canvas of={Stories.FaceOpen} />

**Story:** "Change it right where it is." The card stays in its column and grows: length and
status are on its face, with what differs from the sveit; "Fleira…" opens the rest under it.

**Hierarchy:** the two edits a leader makes most on the night (length and status) come first
and need no extra step. Everything else is one tap away.

**Why:** the closest thing to writing on the plan. The leader never loses their place: the card
they pressed is the card they're editing.

**Trade-offs:** an open card pushes the rest of its column down, so that one column's time scale
pauses while it's open (the column widens to 17rem so the controls fit; on a phone it takes the
screen's width). Two cards can't be compared while one is open. "Fleira" makes the card long.

### K2 · Flip it over

<Canvas of={Stories.FlipOpen} />

**Story:** "The back of the card is where you write." Pressing the card turns it over (a quarter
turn, none under reduced motion); its picture and name stay at the top of the back, so it is
still the same card. The back is ruled like a playing card's and holds the whole editor.

**Hierarchy:** the card's identity (picture, flokkur, name, "Lokið"), then what differs from the
sveit, then the fields in the order a leader thinks of them, then the moves, then Fjarlægja.

**Why:** a clean split between reading (front) and changing (back). The front never carries
controls, so the grid stays calm.

**Trade-offs:** like K1, the open column's time scale pauses and the column widens. While a card
is turned over its front (time, stamp) is hidden, and the flip is a gimmick if it's slow, so it
is 260ms and only when motion is welcome. Focus moves to the back's heading.

### K3 · Editor panel

<Canvas of={Stories.PanelOpen} />

**Story:** "The grid is the plan; the panel is the desk." The grid never changes shape. The open
card is outlined in place, and a panel beside the grid (20rem, sticky) is headed by that same
card: its picture, "Refir · 19:55–20:10", its name.

**Hierarchy:** the grid keeps the room it needs to compare flokkar; the panel is secondary,
beside it, and gone on "Lokið".

**Why:** the time scale never pauses, so a leader can change Refir's length and watch the whole
band rebalance ("Lengsti flokkur ræður") as they press +. It is also the only direction where
the editor has a fixed, generous width.

**Trade-offs:** the grid gets narrower while the panel is open (four columns at 1280px is fine;
six get tight). On a phone the panel becomes a sheet stuck to the bottom of the screen, at most
55% of its height, over the grid: the leader sees the card's column above it but not the rest.

### K4 · Follows the sveit

<Canvas of={Stories.SyncOpen} />

**Story:** "One plan for the sveit, adapted per flokkur." Every card says whether it is the
sveit's version ("⛓ Sveitin"), changed for this flokkur ("✎ BREYTT"), or only this flokkur's
("Aðeins hér"), and the header counts the changed ones ("2 spjöld breytt fyrir flokk"). The
editor (K3's, under the grid) starts with that status and adds one choice: **Breytingar gilda:
aðeins þessi flokkur (Refir), or hjá öllum sem fylgja sveitinni (3 flokkar).**

**Hierarchy:** sync state first, on every card; the scope choice above the fields, because it
decides what every field does.

**Why:** this is what a stöðvaleikur actually needs. The station leader changes Hnútar to 20
minutes once, and every flokkur that does Hnútar gets it, except Birnir, who have their own
leader for it and keep their version. "Flokkur sem hefur sína eigin útgáfu heldur henni, hvað sem
þú velur hér." Nothing a leader adapted for one flokkur is overwritten from another column.

**Trade-offs:** the most to learn: a second idea (the sveit's version) on top of the plan, and a
scope choice on every edit (it starts at "aðeins þessi flokkur", the safe one, each time a card opens). It
needs the most from the API (below).

## 5. "Sameina í eitt" that says what it keeps

<Canvas of={Stories.Merge} />

Unchanged from the earlier style lab. Today the button keeps the first flokkur's lane and drops
the others in silence.

1. **Lanes still identical** (for example right after a split): nothing is lost, so it merges at
   once, with "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds (UX rule 5).
2. **Lanes that differ:** the button reads "Sameina í eitt…" and opens an inline confirmation,
   which takes focus when the leader opens it.
   - **Halda öllu, hvað á eftir öðru** (the default) keeps every distinct liður once, in the
     first flokkur's order, then whatever the others had beyond that. The preview says which
     flokkar had each liður and what it costs: "6 liðir í einni röð, 85 mín í stað 75 mín
     samhliða. Fundurinn lengist um 10 mín."
   - **Halda einni dagskrá** plus a choice of flokkur. A red list shows what goes, "Fellur út: 3
     liðir · 40 mín", and the button turns red: "Sameina og sleppa 3 liðum".
3. "Hætta við" comes first, is quiet, and returns focus to the button. After merging,
   "Afturkalla" stays for 10 seconds, and the announcement says how many liðir were kept and
   dropped.

**Why keep everything by default:** it's the only choice that never deletes what a leader wrote.
Its cost, a longer evening, is shown in minutes before they confirm, and shows up again in the
sheet's total afterwards. To ship it, `mergeBand` needs a `keep: "all" | patrolId` argument, as
`labMerge` has here.

## 6. What's real and what's fixture

| What                                   | Where it lives today                                                                                | Status                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Length, status, order, flokkur, remove | `Lidur.minutes`, `status`; `benchReducer` (`lanePatch`, `laneMove`, `laneMoveAcross`, `laneRemove`) | Real in the bench contract, on fixtures until the bench backend exists (sc-289–293).                                        |
| The card's picture, kind, theme        | `LibraryBlock` (`image`, `kind`, `theme`)                                                           | Fixture bank; the lab's station blocks are in `cardModel.ts`.                                                               |
| Which bank block a liður is            | Lab-only `block_id` on the liður                                                                    | **Gap.** `Lidur` has no link to the content it came from.                                                                   |
| Who leads it                           | Lab-only `leader`                                                                                   | **Gap.** No field.                                                                                                          |
| Adaptation for this flokkur            | Lab-only `note`                                                                                     | **Gap.** No field (`endurmat` is last time's, not this flokkur's plan).                                                     |
| Búnaður per liður                      | Lab-only `gear`                                                                                     | **Gap.** Bank content has `equipment: list[str]`; it isn't copied to a liður (see Lab/PlanGrid).                            |
| The sveit's version                    | Lab: what most flokkar have when the band is drawn (`troopVersions`)                                | **Gap.** A lane's liður has no link to the band liður it was copied from, and there is no "changed for this flokkur" state. |
| Edit for every follower (K4)           | Lab: one `lanePatch` per follower                                                                   | Needs a server-side "apply to followers" or the client must send N patches.                                                 |
| Merge "keep everything"                | `labMerge`                                                                                          | `mergeBand` needs `keep: "all" \| patrolId`.                                                                                |

The gaps are on Shortcut sc-646.
