# UserManagement: C · Split

The users section of `/admin`, for platform admins. Picked as **C · Split** in the Lab on
2026-10-19 and promoted from `Lab/UserManagement` on 2026-10-20 (sc-640). The other directions (A
Table, B Cards, D Bulk) were removed.

Code: `UserManagement.tsx` (the page: toolbar, summary, pages, confirm and undo),
`UserSplit.tsx` (the list and the panel, and the keyboard), `UserDetail.tsx` (one person),
`ConfirmDialog.tsx`, `UndoToast.tsx`, `UserStates.tsx` (loading, error, empty, no match),
`useUserDirectory.ts` (the data), `useUserListUrl.ts` (the URL) and `userRules.ts` (copy, rules,
search and errors, unit tested).

## The story it tells

An admin comes here to do one thing to one person: make a leader an umsjónarmaður dagskrár, give
someone editing rights in the bank, or remove an account. The list is for finding that person;
the panel is for looking at them properly before changing anything. Side by side, the admin sees
whom they are changing while they change it.

## What the old table got wrong

- **A role was saved the moment the `<select>` changed**, with no confirm. On Windows, arrowing
  a closed select changes it, so an admin could promote someone by accident.
- **No feedback and no undo**: a bare "…" while saving, then nothing.
- **Delete was the only action**, a red word on every row.
- **No permission filter**, search only from two letters, and nothing in the URL.
- **Raw English errors** ("Failed to update user: 500 - …") and no way to retry.
- **24–28px targets** and a small select font, which iOS zooms into.
- **The own row was disabled with no reason.**

## Hierarchy

1. **Search and filter** on top. Search ignores case and accents ("astros" finds "Ástrós"); the
   chips filter by permission and show how many each has.
2. **The list**: avatar, name, email, permission. One Tab stop; ↑ ↓ Home End move inside it. The
   selected row is tinted _and_ has a bar, so it doesn't rely on colour alone.
3. **The panel**, top to bottom: who (name, email, permission), **Réttindi**, **Aðgangur að
   dagskrárbanka**, then **Eyða notanda** last and quiet.

## Why it works this way

- **Saved on purpose.** Each section has its own save button and saves only what changed; with
  nothing changed it says "Engin breyting til að vista." instead of saving (UX rule 6). The hint
  next to the button reads "Meðlimur → Skoðandi, óvistað" so the admin sees the change before
  they make it.
- **Confirm when it grants power** (UX rule 5): a promotion to umsjónarmaður dagskrár, and making
  someone a bank admin. Demotions and smaller steps don't ask.
- **Afturkalla instead of asking twice.** Every saved role change says what happened and offers
  "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds. The one exception: giving a role in the bank to someone who had
  none, because the API can't take a membership away again.
- **Delete is the only permanent action**, so it's a named dialog ("Aron Freyr Kristjánsson
  (aron@…) verður eytt úr Slóða. Ekki er hægt að afturkalla þetta."), with "Hætta við" first and
  focused. A failed delete keeps the dialog open with the reason.
- **Read-only, with the reason.** The admin's own permission ("Annar stjórnandi þarf að gera
  það.") and other platform admins ("skilgreindir í stillingum kerfisins") can't be changed here,
  and the panel says why rather than greying things out (sc-535: admin comes from ADMIN_EMAILS).
- **Errors in the house voice**, chosen from the HTTP status (`userErrorMessage`); the backend's
  English never shows. A failed load has "Reyna aftur".
- **The URL** keeps the search, filter, page and person (`?q=&rettindi=&sida=&notandi=`, with
  `replace`, UX rule 10), so a refresh or a shared link opens the same view.
- **On a phone** the panel replaces the list, with "Til baka í lista"; focus moves to the name
  and back to the row. Targets are 44px, 48px on a phone; inputs are 16px.

## Real data, and what's missing

Everything shown comes from the API today:

- `GET /users/admin/list`, paged through in pages of 200 (`listAllUsers`), so search, filter and
  counts cover everyone rather than one page.
- `PATCH /users/{id}` for the permission, `DELETE /users/{id}` for delete.
- `GET` and `PUT /workspaces/{bank}/members…` for the bank role. The bank section appears once
  those roles have loaded, and is left out if they fail or no bank is set up.

The Lab's version also had **freeze and reinstate, join and last-seen dates, and a change
history**. None of these is in the API, so they are left out here rather than faked; each place
they'd go is marked `TODO(sc-640)`.

## Trade-offs

- Loading every user is fine at Slóði's size (hundreds). At many thousands, search and the counts
  would have to move to the backend.
- The confirm dialog is a native `<dialog>` of its own rather than the shared `Modal`, which has
  no focus trap or focus return yet.
