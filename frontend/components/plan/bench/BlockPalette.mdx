# BlockPalette: C · Card grid with C2 · Covers

The BlockPalette is the dagskrárbankinn rail on the left of the Vinnubekkur, where a leader picks
blocks for a fundur: collections at the top, blocks below them, the plan in the main body, all in
PlanShell's 296px column. Picked in the Lab (C · Card grid on 2026-10-19, C2 · Covers for the
collections on 2026-10-20) and promoted from `Lab/BlockPalette`. The leader's own collections are
**folders (möppur)**; in the product they are called _möppur_, never "albúm".

Code: `BlockPalette.tsx` (the rail, the cards and "Setja í möppu…"), `PaletteFolders.tsx` (the
sources, the covers, the popover and the name field), `blockIcons.ts` (every icon and why),
`hooks/useFolders.ts` (the möppur, in memory until sc-620) and the fixtures in
`lib/mock/library.mock.ts` and `lib/mock/folders.mock.ts`.

## Why C · Card grid

- **The picture does the telling-apart.** A 64px tile (the block's image, or its theme's icon on
  a tint of its type) is big enough to read, so the leader scans by picture before reading.
- **It's for browsing.** A leader opens the rail most often not knowing yet what they want. Cards
  show enough to judge without opening anything.
- **It suits folders.** A grid of pictures already looks like a folder, so a collection that's
  "ours" feels like a place, not a filter.

### What changed on the card

- **No side stripe.** The coloured edge was decoration standing in for meaning. The type now
  shows as an icon and its word ("Leikur", with dice), and as the tint behind the picture. The
  card's border is neutral.
- **The length is a clock and "15 mín"** under the title, in body ink (`BlockMinutes`, shared
  with the SplitGrid lab's cards). It used to be a white chip over the picture's corner, which was
  hard to read on the light tiles and covered the picture.
- **The title wraps and hyphenates** (`lang="is"`, `hyphens: auto`). It never truncates, because
  the end of an Icelandic title is often what tells two blocks apart.
- **Badges with icons:** "Þegar á fundinum" (a tick) and "Endurmat fylgir" (a note). They sit on a
  neutral tint, never coloured.
- **"Bæta við" plus a bookmark button** ("Setja í möppu…"). Both are 44px. The bookmark is filled
  when the block is in a folder, and its accessible name says how many ("í 2 möppum").
- **The picture is the drag handle**, marked with a grip. It's the biggest thing on the card,
  and "Bæta við" stays a plain click.
- **The six type chips became one select** ("Allar tegundir"), beside the search. The chips took
  two rows the cards needed once the collections grew.

## Folders

A **folder** (_mappa_) is a named list of blocks the leader keeps for a reason of their own: "Uppáhalds
leikir", "Rigningardagar", "Útilega haust", "Fyrir Drekaskáta". It holds **references, not
copies**: editing a block in the bank changes it in every folder, and a block can be in several.

How folders differ from the built-in sources:

- **Sources say where a block comes from.** They are fixed and can't be edited. _Allt_ is the whole
  bank. _Félagið_ is what's shared in the félag. _Færnimerki_ is the badge programme. _Sveitin mín_
  is what the sveit made itself.
- **Folders say what a block is for.** They are made, named, ordered and deleted by the leader. A
  Félagið block and a Sveitin mín block can sit side by side in "Rigningardagar".
- **Sveitin mín stays a source.** It's authorship, not curation, so it's one of the source tabs
  and can't be renamed or deleted.

### Personal or shared with the sveit

The proposal is two owners:

- **Personal** (owned by a user): the default. "Mín uppáhalds" belongs to one foringi, and nobody
  else needs to see it.
- **Shared with the sveit** (owned by the workspace): every foringi in the sveit sees it and can add
  to it. That's the natural home for "Rigningardagar" or "Útilega haust", which the whole team
  builds up. It's marked with a people icon and "deilt með sveitinni" for screen readers.

A new folder is personal. Sharing it is one deliberate step (a later "Deila með sveitinni"
action), never a side effect. Who may edit a shared folder follows the workspace role: an
editor or above can add and remove blocks, and only the folder's creator or an admin can rename
or delete it.

## Folder behaviour

- **Create:** "+ Ný mappa" opens an inline name field. Enter creates the folder and selects it,
  and Escape cancels. A duplicate name (trimmed, any case) is refused with "Þú átt þegar möppu sem
  heitir „…“", announced, and the typed text is kept. The limit is 60 characters.
- **Rename:** inline, with the same rules.
- **Delete:** at once, with no dialog, and "Afturkalla" for 10 seconds (rule 5: reversible acts
  don't confirm).
- **Add a block from its card:** the bookmark opens "Setja í möppu", with every folder as a ticked
  or unticked checkbox and "+ Ný mappa" (which creates the folder with the block already in it).
- **Empty folder:** "Mappan „Jólafundur“ er tóm.", how to fill it, and "Sýna allan bankann".
- **Counts** go through `countIs`: "3 blokkir", "1 blokk", "14 möppur".
- **Feedback:** every change is said once in the rail's live region (rule 4).
- **Popovers** use the native `popover` attribute, so they're never clipped by the scrolling
  list. Escape closes them, and focus goes back to the button that opened them.
- **Selection** is a toggle button with `aria-pressed`, as in the current rail.

## The covers: folders you can see into

**The story.** The sources are a compact row of four tabs with icons. The folders are covers: a
2 × 2 mosaic of the first four blocks inside, or the folder's icon while it's empty. Three
across, with the name and count under each.

**Hierarchy.** Cover picture → name → count. The selected folder's actions (Endurnefna, Eyða
möppu) appear as words in a bar under the covers, because a cover is too small for a "…".

**Why.** It makes the folders the thing you see. A mosaic tells "Útilega haust" (fire, tent) from
"Rigningardagar" (flag, knots, heart) without reading, and it echoes the card grid below.

**Trade-offs.** Names wrap at about 80px, so long names make tall covers. Six covers is the most
that shows before scrolling. The actions are one step further away (select, then act), and the
mosaic is only as good as the pictures in it.

## The data it needs

Nothing here exists in the API yet: the bank and the möppur are both fixtures, shown in mock
mode only. `useFolders` keeps the möppur in memory, and its `TODO(sc-620)` is where the API plugs
in. Favourites are hidden behind `FAVOURITES_ENABLED` (off), and
**sc-620** ("Build real favourites: backend table + endpoints, 'Mappan mín' in the sidebar") is
where they get a backend. The proposal: folders **are** the favourites model, generalised.
"Uppáhalds" is one folder every user gets by default, and sc-620's _Mappan mín_ is the list of a
user's folders. One model then serves the sidebar and the rail.

### Tables

**`folder`**

- `id`: uuid
- `owner_user_id`: uuid, nullable. Set for a personal folder.
- `owner_workspace_id`: uuid, nullable. Set for a folder shared with the sveit. Exactly one of the
  two owners is set (a check constraint).
- `name`: text, 1–60 characters. Unique per owner, case-insensitive (`lower(name)`), so the
  duplicate error can come from the server too.
- `icon`: text, one of a fixed set (`bookmark`, `star`, `cloud-rain`, `leaf`, `snowflake`, `flame`).
- `position`: integer, the order in the owner's list.
- `is_default`: bool. The one "Uppáhalds" folder per user, which can't be deleted.
- `created_by`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, and `deleted_at` (soft delete, `SoftDeleteMixin`).
  Soft delete is what makes "Afturkalla" a restore rather than a re-create.

**`folder_item`**

- `folder_id`: fk, on delete cascade.
- `content_id`: fk to `content` (Program, Event, Task), so a bank item of any kind can be put in a
  folder, not just a block.
- `position`: integer, the order inside the folder (the covers' mosaic shows the first four).
- `added_by`, `added_at`.
- Primary key `(folder_id, content_id)`: a block is in a folder at most once.

### Endpoints

- `GET /folders?workspace_id=…`: the caller's own folders plus those shared in the workspace,
  ordered by `position`, each with `item_count` and up to four `cover_items` (id, theme, kind,
  image) for the covers.
- `POST /folders` with `{ name, icon?, workspace_id? }`: create. `workspace_id` makes it shared.
  A duplicate name returns 409.
- `PATCH /folders/{id}` with `{ name?, icon?, position? }`: rename or reorder. Reordering one
  folder shifts the others on the server.
- `DELETE /folders/{id}`: soft delete. `POST /folders/{id}/restore` is "Afturkalla", within the
  undo window.
- `GET /folders/{id}/items`: the items, ordered.
- `PUT /folders/{id}/items/{content_id}` / `DELETE …`: put a block in or take it out. Both are
  idempotent, which suits a checkbox.
- `GET /content/{id}/folders`: which of the caller's folders an item is in. This is what the card's
  picker ticks, and it could be folded into the bank list as `folder_ids` to save a request per card.

**Permissions** follow `check_workspace_access`. A personal folder is owner-only. A shared folder
can be read by any workspace member, its items changed by an editor or above, and renamed or
deleted by its creator or a workspace admin.

## Rules the rail follows

- "Bæta við" and dragging from the picture, both through the bench's own intents
  (`blockAsLidur`, `dragId.library`).
- Search, type and the collections stay in the same place. The count line names the selected
  collection: "Uppáhalds leikir: 3 blokkir".
- With no fundur selected, "Bæta við" is disabled, and the reason is visible text linked with
  `aria-describedby` (rule 9). "Setja í möppu…" still works, because folders don't need a fundur.
- 44px targets everywhere, with a visible 2px focus ring. Inputs are 16px.
- `--sl-*` tokens only. Filled controls use the `-text` tier, so they pass AA in light, dark and
  patrol.
- Transitions collapse to 1ms under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Icons

See the _Icon mapping_ story. **Type:** Setning `Sunrise`, Dagskrá `BookOpen`, Leikur `Dices`,
Slit `Moon`, Endurmat `MessagesSquare`, Annað `Puzzle`. **Theme:** Útilíf `Tent`, Ratleikni
`Compass`, Hreyfing `Footprints`, Hefðir `Flag`, Umhyggja `Heart`, Matur `Flame`, Endurmat
`MessagesSquare`, anything else `Shapes`.

Sources and folders use their own icons (Library, Building2, Award, House for the sources; star,
cloud, leaf, snowflake, flame and bookmark for folders), so a collection is never mistaken for a
type or a theme. The one overlap is the flame, which is both the Matur theme and a folder icon.
It's acceptable because a theme icon only appears on a card and a folder icon only in the
collections area.

Colour is never the only carrier: WCAG 1.4.1, the patrol colours already mean age groups, and
shape survives greyscale and colour blindness. The type's colour survives only as the tint behind
the picture.
