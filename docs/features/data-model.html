1. Content is the thing itself

Content is what something is: its name, description, instructions and the rest of today's bank fields. It says nothing about when it happens or what holds it. The programs, events and tasks tables go, and Content becomes one table with a level.

  • A number in the database, names in the code. The column is a small integer; Python and TypeScript use ContentLevel.event, never 1. The Icelandic labels live in one frontend map.
  • The level is a label, not a shape. Every level has the same fields. The bank filters by it and the frontend draws each level its own way.
  • A starfsár is just the top level. There is no Season table.

2. Placements: where and when

A Placement puts content inside another placement, at a time. Slit is one Content placed in 30 fundir, at 19:20 in one and 19:25 in another. Editing Slit changes it in all of them.

  • A placement always has a time. starts_at and ends_at are never empty, so the frontend can always draw where something sits in its parent.
  • time_set says whether the user chose the time. While it is false, the frontend placed it and the time is hidden: dragging moves it but keeps it hidden. Typing a time sets it to true and shows it. A scratchpad is mostly hidden times, a plan mostly set ones; they are the same table.
  • The parent is a placement, not content. A fundur placed on two dates has its own verkefni on each, so moving Setning on one date leaves the other alone.
  • One rule on levels: a placement's content has a lower level than its parent's content. A level may be skipped (a starfsár can hold a fundur directly), but nothing holds its own level, so nothing can end up holding itself. The database enforces it with composite foreign keys and a check constraint, no trigger; the service checks first so the error is readable.
  • Moving something moves what it holds. Moving a fundur shifts its verkefni; moving a starfsár shifts its fundir. The service does this as one update.
  • Children may run over their parent. Patrols can run verkefni side by side, and a plan is a draft. The frontend warns („15 mín umfram“) instead of refusing.
  • A placement stays in one workspace. Its content and its parent placement belong to the same workspace. Content from another workspace, the bank included, is copied in before it is placed.
  • Placing an item fills its length from duration_min, the bank's estimate. The leader can change it.
  • Deleting a placement takes the item out of its parent, with the placements it holds, and leaves the content. Deleting content deletes its placements.

3. Workspaces

A workspace holds content, placements and units. The bank, a plan and a scratchpad are all workspaces; they differ in purpose and members, not in structure. Any editor in a workspace can edit all of its content and placements. A plan made in a scratchpad is copied to another workspace the same way a bank idea is.

  • An idea with structure is content with one placement at the top. „Dagskrárhringur um rötun“ is a Content whose placement in the bank holds three fundir, each holding its verkefni. Its hidden times only give the layout: fundur 1 in week 1, Setning before Verkefni. A different layout is a copy with its own name, so an idea has one structure.
  • A single idea needs no placement. A verkefni on its own is just content in the bank, as today.
  • The bank's times start from a fixed day, Monday 3 January 2000, so weeks and weekdays mean something („vika 2, þriðjudagur“).
  • Using a bank idea copies it, its content and its structure, into your workspace, shifted in whole weeks to the date you pick so the weekdays stay. A copy means the author's edits, or a moderator hiding the item, never change someone's plan.
  • copied_from_id remembers where a copy came from. When an idea holds content you already have a copy of, the user chooses: „Nota þitt eða nýtt afrit?“, defaulting to yours. Copying an idea you already have works the same way: using yours places it again with its own placements; a new copy gets the bank's current version. Deleting the original leaves the copy (ON DELETE SET NULL).
  • Inside the bank, ideas share content. When B holds A, edits to A show in B. If A is hidden, B shows a greyed gap where A was. B's author can make their own version of A to freeze it.
  • No stored meeting schedule. The workspace's default_meeting_weekday, default_start_time, default_end_time, default_interval and season_start are dropped; nothing reads them. Repeating a fundur is a frontend tool („copy weekly until …“) that creates placements once, not a rule in the data.

4. Units

  • Unit is any group of people in a workspace. It has no type: a unit holds other units, and the user decides what each level means (deild, sveit, flokkur, or something else). A workspace can have as many units as it needs.
  • Units have nothing to do with users. A unit is who the content is for, not who can edit it; editing rights come from workspace membership.
  • Every unit has a colour. It gets a default from a palette when it's created, and the user can change it. Stored as #rrggbb, checked on write. The frontend picks white or dark text from its lightness so it stays readable in both themes.
  • UnitParticipation says which units a placement is for, so the same verkefni can be for Ernir in one fundur and for Fálkar in another. A placement with none is for whoever its parent is for. It replaces troop_participation.

5. Comments, tags, likes and moderation

  • Comments stay on content, as today. Endurmat written after a fundur is a comment on the content, so whoever runs it next time sees how it went.
  • Tags, likes, reports and review comments stay on content, unchanged.
  • Yfirferð only reads the bank. The moderation queue filters on the bank workspace, so content in plans and scratchpads never lands there. Today it reads every workspace, so this has to ship with placements.

6. What goes

  • The programs, events and tasks tables, content_type, Event.program_id, Task.event_id, Event.start_dt and end_dt. The migration sets level from content_type and turns each old parent pointer and event date into a placement.
  • The troops and troop_participation tables, replaced by units.
  • The polymorphic models and the ProgramOut/EventOut/TaskOut schemas and routes collapse into one. A separate PR from the bench.

Parked

  • Collection: a Pinterest-style board of content, with no times and no order. It only references content and holds no structure.
  • Whether editing content changes past fundir. For now it does; later the leader may choose, and set a default.
  • Yfirferð for ideas with structure. Leaning towards: B can be approved while content it holds is still waiting.
  • Unit order. No position for now; add one if the frontend needs to order columns by hand.
  • Groups. A group lets users be in a group, and a group can own workspaces. Workspace.group_id and the groups and group_memberships tables stay as they are; nothing here builds on them.

Left out of the diagram on purpose: posting suspensions, email drafts and the email list, and the games. They don't touch content.
