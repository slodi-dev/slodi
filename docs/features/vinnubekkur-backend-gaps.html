Replaced (16. nóvember 2026): kept for history. The current model is in data-model.html and the endpoints are in plan-api.html; an updated gap map follows.

Every action in the Vinnubekkur stories, matched against the backend on origin/main. The goal is to find what the frontend wants that the backend doesn't have yet.

5. What stands out

  1. Season is the root of everything, and it doesn't exist. Every view hangs off selectedId. Until Season and its two routes are built, the whole route runs on fixtures. This is the first backend task.
  2. The bench has no contract. The grid at least has a spec in plan-api-contract.md. The 21 bench intents have only a TypeScript type, PlanBenchData, so they need a contract before anyone writes a backend for them.
  3. A liður can't simply be a Task. Task is a subtype of Content, so every liður a leader adds would become a public bank item and land in the Yfirferð queue. A liður probably needs its own table that references a Content block, which also gives it the missing position, status, kind and patrol-lane columns.
  4. The Rist already has half its data. Event, Troop and TroopParticipation cover "which patrol is at which event" and start_dt. What's missing is scope, type, status (including unknown, the „?“ mark) and the link to a season.
  5. One quick win on the frontend. The bank rail could read GET /workspaces/{id}/content today, without folders and use counts, instead of fixtures.
  6. Nothing is saved. Even with fixtures replaced, the bench has no write path, so the edit and autosave work in A3 depends on items 2 and 3.
  7. Small wiring gaps on the frontend. PlanGrid accepts onSelectEvent but PlanShell doesn't pass it, so clicking a Rist entry does nothing. useCreateSeason has no button.
