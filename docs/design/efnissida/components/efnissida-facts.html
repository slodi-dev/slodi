EfnissidaFacts

The six facts a leader decides on, in three presentations. Reshaped from ProgramQuickInfo.

A <dl>, not a table: each fact is a dt label and a dd value. Empty facts are omitted, never rendered as „—" — six dashes tell the reader nothing and push the real values down. Age bands carry patrol colour and their label, same as the create modal.
