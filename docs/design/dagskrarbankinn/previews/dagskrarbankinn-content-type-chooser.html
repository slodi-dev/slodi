ContentTypeChooser

The bank's create launcher. Four kinds of thing now, not three — and for the September release only Verkefni can be created. Source: docs/content-model.md.

The model changed, so the menu has to. The bank holds four kinds of thing, and the difference between them is what they contain: a Verkefni is one dagskrárliður and contains nothing; a Fundur is a collection of verkefni; a Dagskrárhringur is a selection of fundir; a Viðburður is also a collection of verkefni, but spread over several days rather than one evening. Two of them are collections of the same thing and only time separates them, which is precisely why the hint lines cannot be dropped.

Three are shown and disabled, not hidden. A chooser with one option says the bank only ever does one thing. A leader needs to see that it will hold their fundur eventually. The reason all three are off is the same: they are collections, and nothing can be put into a collection yet — no screen fills one — so creating one today makes an empty container its author cannot fill and a reviewer cannot judge.

Three decisions to sanity-check

The seam reads as a stray line

The hairline between sheet and button is --sl-color-primary-text, matching the button. In dark mode that token is pale green, so on a dark card it shows as a near-white rule across the component. Either drop it in dark or take it from --sl-color-border.

Disabled items stay focusable

aria-disabled rather than disabled, so a screen-reader user still meets all four and learns the bank will hold them. A removed option teaches nothing; a skipped one teaches nothing either.

Four options is a taller sheet

At 4 × ~84px plus the title the sheet is ~370px. On a short phone in landscape that plus the 56px button is most of the viewport — it may need to cap its height and scroll, or drop to a full-height sheet on mobile.

Order

Fixed, and it is the model read out loud: Verkefni (one thing) → Fundur (a collection of them) → Dagskrárhringur (a collection of those) → Viðburður (a collection of verkefni again, but across days). The one thing that can be created sits first, where the focus lands. Viðburður last because it breaks the widening sequence — it belongs beside Fundur logically, but putting it there would push the two disabled collection kinds apart and make the list look arbitrary.
